# ferrofluid: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Style, Spike Size, Input 3 Holds, Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Height, Tilt, Picture, Strength, By Brightness, By Nearness, Magnet, Sensitivity, Max Distance, Bass and Beat Pulse; a Toggle for Full Screen (a state); Pulses for Reset, Open Window and Open Docs; an Int for Display. Labels in Title Case (Input 3 Holds keeps its small word lower case). The longest names are Openwindow, Fullscreen, Depthtype and Beatpulse (10 or fewer; the save check passes).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: drive reads By Brightness, By Nearness (only while input 3 is wired), Magnet, Spike Size (its grid and the picture's mip level), Strength, Bass (times bass_lag) and Beat Pulse (times beat_env) while input 2 is wired, and the magnet's place from magnet_lag (or the clock unwired); look reads Style, Picture, Height and Spike Size (the spikes' height in pixels) and Tilt; find reads Input 3 Holds and Sensitivity, near Depth Type and Max Distance. The Parameter Execute DAT reset only pulses height_fb on Reset, copying no values. Values only the inside needs (the spikes' spacing in cells, the step, the steps a second, the quadratic term, r flat and driven, the noise, the softness, the heights at Height 0 and 1, the bass's rise and fall, the beat's lift and time, the magnet's size, path and timing, the look's reflectance, softbox, bend and highlight) are constants in the builder or the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Style, Spike Size, Height, Tilt and Picture (the look); Strength, By Brightness, By Nearness, Magnet and Reset (the field); Input 3 Holds, Sensitivity, Depth Type and Max Distance (input 3); Bass and Beat Pulse (the music); then Flashing and the window. Picture grays out unless Style is Black (Tinted shows the whole picture), Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. The input-3 and music parameters stay live with nothing wired, as no library effect grays a parameter by its wiring; their help says they act on input 3 or input 2.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and bar_count, and steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame each rises. Beats go through flash_gate() (ask, flash_limit, flash) to beat_env. Bars go through bar_gap (a Trigger CHOP, at most one every 2 s) to bars (a Count CHOP: the magnet's steps since the effect started, so wiring a signal in mid-song doesn't send the magnet through every bar so far) and magnet_lag (eased 1.5 s). bass_lag eases the bass (up 0.05 s, down 0.3 s). The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, the audio terms fall away (WIRED: audio's source is 0): the field at Strength, no jumps, the magnet drifting on the clock.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The height field takes RATE (1200) steps a second: field's Passes follow absTime.stepSeconds (at most 0.1 s, 40 steps), so a dropped frame doesn't slow the spikes. bass_lag, beat_env, bar_gap and magnet_lag are in seconds; the unwired magnet drifts on absTime through math.sin and math.cos; the noise's salt is clock_seconds(), never the raw clock.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 245 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 24 and 38.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  seed and field are 32-bit float (the height, stepped 20 times a frame by small amounts, and the noise a hundredth of a spike); drive, shape, near and find are 16-bit float (the growth rate, the height and its peaks, nearness); surface is one channel of 16-bit float (the cones' height, read by the look's march); look is 8-bit (the picture); clear is a 1 x 1 32-bit Constant (where the height starts).
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs on the Mac otherwise idle (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, the dancer's depth on input 3, stress beat, the demo paused: GPU 3.11, 2.99, 2.87 ms (95th percentile 3.64, 3.60, 3.56), CPU 0.54, 0.54, 0.55; 60 fps held every time. The page gives the medians. Costliest: field 1.34 to 1.47 ms (20 passes), look 0.77, surface 0.55, find 0.22. Spike Size Small, one run: GPU 4.38 ms (field 3.23). Three earlier runs while the Mac was indexing after an update read 1.96 to 2.02 ms, a sign of how far this GPU's times wander with its clock; the page gives the idle runs. Later builds changed only the README DAT's text and Spike Size's help.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new ferrofluid.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Cowley and Rosensweig (J. Fluid Mech. 1967) for the phenomenon, Gollwitzer, Rehberg and Richter (J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 2006, linked; checked against its abstract: the bifurcation to hexagons and the hysteretic turn to squares, which this effect doesn't reach), Swift and Hohenberg (Phys. Rev. A 1977) for the equation, Oono and Puri (Phys. Rev. A 1988) for the isotropic Laplacian, steep parallax mapping (Kaneko et al. 2001; McGuire and McGuire 2005), Schlick's Fresnel approximation (1994) and Blinn-Phong (1977) for the view, the library's depth reading, Catmull-Rom resampling, Chris Wellons' lowbias32 hash (public domain, linked) and red guard, Dixon (2001) for the beat clock's idea, WCAG 2.3.1 and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). field.glsl, look.glsl, surface.glsl and shape.glsl name their sources where the methods sit. The docs page says what came before (ferrofluid hardware driven from TouchDesigner, web shaders of cones on a fixed grid; none takes a picture), the patents searched and how it keeps clear (Adobe US 11,409,423 and US 11,755,187, Huawei's pending US 2025/0117558, all Not used; private/patents/ferrofluid.md), and what was tried and set aside (a stronger quadratic term, raw nearness, the straight-on view as default). Original, after a search: the picture and the depth as the field, the wandering magnet on the bars, the cones from the field's peaks, the picture through the flat liquid.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py, worst second by the area rule (Off, Calm, Up to 3 per second), red where any, against the clip alone, stress beat (8 beats, 2 bars, 1 drop a second): dancer Black 0.5, 0.5, 0.5 and Tinted 1.0, 1.0, 1.0 (clip 1.0); flowers Black 0.5, 0.5, 0.5 and Tinted 1.0, 1.0, 1.0 (1.0 r1.0); plaza 0, 0, 0 (0.5); hands 1.0, 1.0, 1.0 (1.5); koi 0.5, 0.5, 0.5 (0.5 r1.5); no red flashes anywhere. Corner on the flowers (Strength 2, Beat Pulse 1, Height 1, Spike Size Small): 1.0, 1.0, 1.0. The Tinted runs are on the final look; the Black runs predate only comment changes and Tinted's base.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color. The spikes read by their shape, their dark and their highlights; Tinted's colors are the picture's own. Every pixel goes through the red guard. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Raw audio's beat clock is labeled experimental in the README DAT (AUDIO, EXPERIMENTAL) and on the docs page (Experimental). The rest is deterministic, the noise hashed.
