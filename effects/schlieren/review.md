# schlieren: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Style, Input 3 Holds, Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Strength, Edge Angle, Fringes, Backdrop, Heat, Rise, Swirl, Linger, Stir, Warm by Movement, Warm by Nearness, Warm by Light, Sensitivity, Max Distance, Beat Puffs, Loudness, Band Heights and Bar Turn; Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Pulses for Reset, Open Window and Open Docs; an Int for Display. Labels in Title Case (Warm by Movement, Input 3 Holds keep their small words lower case). The longest names are Openwindow, Fullscreen, Depthtype and Warmlight (10 or fewer; the save check passes).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: look reads Style, Strength, Backdrop, Fringes and Edge Angle (plus Bar Turn times turn_lag when input 2 is wired); air reads Rise, Swirl, Stir and Beat Puffs (times puff_env and Song Moments' drop_env); heat reads Heat, Linger, Loudness (times level_smooth), Band Heights (bands_smooth) and Beat Puffs; subject reads Warm by Nearness (only while input 3 is wired) and Warm by Light; warmth Warm by Movement; find reads Input 3 Holds and Sensitivity, near Depth Type and Max Distance. The Parameter Execute DAT reset only pulses the Feedback TOPs on Reset, copying no values. Values only the inside needs (the lift's reach and noise, the velocity's half-life and speed cap, the pressure passes, the heat's rate and cooling at 0.5, the puff's lift, heat and area, the warmth's thresholds and times, the refraction, the turn's gap and easing) are constants in the builder or the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Style, Strength, Edge Angle, Fringes and Backdrop (the look); Heat, Rise, Swirl, Linger, Stir and Reset (the air); Warm by Movement, Warm by Nearness, Warm by Light, Input 3 Holds, Sensitivity, Depth Type and Max Distance (what warms it, input 3); Beat Puffs, Loudness, Band Heights, Bar Turn and Song Moments (the music); then Flashing and the window. Edge Angle and Bar Turn gray out unless Style is Knife Edge or Rainbow, Fringes unless it is Interferogram, Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. The input-3 and music parameters stay live with nothing wired, as no library effect grays a parameter by its wiring; their help says they act on input 3 or input 2.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, drop_count and bar_count, and steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame each rises. Beats and drops go through flash_gate() (ask, flash_limit, flash) to puff_env and drop_env. Bars go through bar_gap (a Trigger CHOP, at most one every 2 s) to bars (a Count CHOP: the turns since the effect started, so wiring a signal in mid-song doesn't spin the blade through every bar so far) and turn_lag (eased 1.5 s). level_smooth eases the loudness (half a second) and bands_smooth the seven bands (a sixth of a second). The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, the audio terms fall away (WIRED: audio's source is 0): no puffs, no turn, the bands even.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The air and the heat step by the frame's step (absTime.stepSeconds, at most 0.05 s): velocities are cells a second, the lift and puffs cells a second squared, the heating and cooling rates a second; the warmth rises and falls by it (lib warmth()); puff_env, drop_env, bar_gap and turn_lag are in seconds; the unwired light circles on absTime through math.sin. The lift's noise drifts with clock_seconds(), never the raw clock. Movement comes from the input's new frames, held between them (lib flow_speed(), warmth()).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 292 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 56 and 73.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  air and lib incompressible's passes are 32-bit float (velocities in cells a second and the pressure, whose small differences move the air); subject, lift, heat, bend, near and find are 16-bit float (fractions 0-1, the heat that cools by a fraction a frame, the bending, nearness); look is 8-bit (the picture); clear is a 1 x 1 32-bit Constant (the start of every Feedback TOP); warm_area is an Analyze TOP's one pixel. The lib passes (optical flow, flow_speed, warmth) keep their reviewed formats.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs on the build with the final solver (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, the dancer's depth on input 3, stress beat, the demo paused: GPU 3.15, 3.09, 2.91 ms (95th percentile 4.14, 4.11, 4.06), CPU 0.74, 0.75, 0.74; 60 fps held every time. The page gives the medians. Costliest: lk8 0.6 to 0.7 ms, pressure 0.6 (20 passes; 30 measured 0.86), look 0.6, then lift, find and warmth about 0.2 each. Later builds changed only constants (the air's speed cap, Rise's scale, the puff's area, the shadowgraph's contrast), no pass's work. The Mac was short of memory this session (15 of 16 GB in use): Blob Track alone read 6.85 to 7.72 ms against its page's 2.28 at the end of it, so these numbers may read high rather than low.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new schlieren.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Settles (Springer 2001, linked) for the optics, Brownlee, Pegoraro, Shankar, McCormick and Hansen (IEEE TVCG 2011, linked) for the four looks from a simulated field (followed, not copied), Stam (SIGGRAPH 1999) and Harris (GPU Gems 2004) for the air with Fluid's shared pressure solve, Fedkiw, Stam and Jensen (SIGGRAPH 2001) for vorticity confinement, Selle, Fedkiw, Kim, Liu and Rossignac (J. Sci. Comput. 2008) for the heat's MacCormack step and clamp, Wyman, Sloan and Shirley (JCGT 2013, linked) for Rainbow's spectrum, the library's red guard, warmth, optical flow and depth reading, Chris Wellons' lowbias32 hash (public domain, linked), Dixon (2001) for the beat clock's idea, WCAG 2.3.1 and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). air.glsl, heat.glsl, bend.glsl and look.glsl name their sources where the methods sit. The docs page says what came before (Brownlee's scientific renderings, heat-haze shaders; no live schlieren effect found for TouchDesigner), the patents searched and how it keeps clear (MIT US 9,710,917 and US 10,636,149, ByteDance US 12,694,597 and US 12,182,906, Huawei's pending US 2025/0117558, all Not used; private/patents/schlieren.md and schlieren_sweep.md), and what was tried and set aside (multiplied relief, noise on the heating, a plain step back for the heat, a lift twice as strong). Original, after a search: the picture as the source of the heat, the rising column above what is warm, the subject and the lights kept opaque, the gray schlieren photograph as a backdrop, the puffs weighted by how much of the picture is warm, the bands by height.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final solver, one run at a time, worst second by the area rule (Off, Calm, Up to 3 per second), red where any, against the clip alone, stress beat (8 beats, 2 bars, 1 drop a second): dancer Knife Edge 1.0, 1.0, 1.0, Rainbow 1.0, 1.0, 1.0, Shadowgraph 1.0, 1.0, 1.0, Interferogram 1.0, 1.0, 1.0 (1.0); flowers 1.0, 2.0, 2.5 (1.0 r1.0); plaza 1.0, 1.5, 1.5 (0.5); hands 1.5, 1.5, 1.5 (1.5); koi 1.0, 1.0, 1.0 (0.5 r1.5); no red flashes anywhere. Corners on the flowers: Strength 1, Rise 1 (Off) and Beat Puffs 1 (Up to 3) each within their rules alone (1.5, 1.5, 2.0); all three together 2.0, 3.5, 3.5, documented on the page as over WCAG's 3 there. On the way (board #4847): puffs flashed a lamp's shifted point 16 times a second in one cell (lights made opaque, the shift capped, puffs halved); the blade turned on every bar added half a flash with Flashing Off (at most one turn every 2 s); the flowers, warm all over, flashed 3.5 at Calm (puffs weighted by how much is warm, the red guard on every pixel); Shadowgraph's 0.6 contrast added half a flash with Flashing Off (0.45); Rise at 1 with twice today's lift added 1.5 with Flashing Off (the speed cap, Rise rescaled). The last builder change (Bar Turn's enableExpr) changes no output.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color. Knife Edge, Shadowgraph and Interferogram are gray relief, lines and fringes; Rainbow's colors come with the relief's brightness and only show the air's shape, which the other styles show without color. Every pixel goes through the red guard. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Raw audio's beat clock and Fosfora's drop detection are labeled experimental in the README DAT (AUDIO, EXPERIMENTAL), on the docs page (Experimental) and in Song Moments' help. The rest is deterministic, the lift's noise hashed.
