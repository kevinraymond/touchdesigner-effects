# slit_scan: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Toggles for Reverse, Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Menus for Map, Film, History Size, Between Frames, Flips and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Delay, Angle, Spin, Center X, Center Y, Scale, Drift, Position, Speed, History, Beat Kick, Loudness and Length; Ints for Bands and Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (History Size, Between Frames, Beat Kick, Song Moments, Center X). The longest names are Loudness, Histsize and Position (8), and the build passes the save-time check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the scan's uniforms (Map and Fill by menuIndex, Bands, Reverse with the flip's slide, Angle plus how far Spin has turned, Center X and Y, Scale and how far Drift has moved), the slit's (Film by menuIndex and Reverse as the film's direction, Position, Speed with loudness under its cap), the rings' Cache Size and frames' width (History x the project's cook rate), the history's resolution (History Size), the flip and freeze requests' gains (Flips, Song Moments), the freeze's length (Length), the delay asked for (Delay with loudness and build), the gate and budget (Flashing), and the tears' depth (Beat Kick). No script copies values. The limits (the delay's glide, Drift x delay, Spin x delay, the flip's slide, Slit's top speed), the tear's size, length and depth, the flip's slide time, the history's slack and gap, and the flash spacing stay in the builder and shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Map to Reverse (the time map), History to Between Frames (the history), Flips to Length (the music), then Flashing, then the window. Delay, Bands and Flips gray out for Slit; Angle and Spin show only for Sweep, Center X and Y for Rings, Scale and Drift for Liquid, Film, Position and Speed for Slit; Length grays out with Song Moments off. Beat Kick and Loudness stay live for every map (both act on Slit too, as their help says).
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, bar_count and drop_count, and a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step. Flips follow bars (or beats), freezes follow drops; drop_kind marks what passed the gate as a drop's, so drop_pulse starts a freeze and flip_pulse (the rest) counts toward flip_n, whose parity the flip slides to. Beat steps (or steady ticks with nothing wired) drive beat_env, which holds the tears open, and beat_n, a Count CHOP of them, which places them. level is read as a level (level_smooth), build as a level. The beat channel is never read.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  Everything follows seconds. The rings write a slot every cook, but each slot's stamp says when (lib clock_seconds(), wrapped every 16384 s) and when its input frame came, and every delay is looked up by those times, so a dropped frame only drops a slot. The film moves by its speed x absTime.stepSeconds (at most 0.1 s). The tears last 0.15 s (a Trigger CHOP decay), the flip's slide, the delay's glide and the freeze run in seconds (Lag CHOP slopes, a Trigger CHOP attack), Spin and Drift are integrated by Speed CHOPs per second, and the steady clock is absTime.seconds. The project's cook rate sizes the rings only (History x cookRate slots).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 109 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 75 and 78. No expression reads another operator's size: the rings' sizes come from History and the project's cook rate, the history's resolution from the History Size menu, and the one-pixel states start from a 2 x 1 constant. The rings only write while pulled, so an unused effect's history goes stale; the frame list sees the gap and the history grows back in.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  pics is 8-bit RGBA, the picture as it came. flows is two 16-bit floats (UV displacements of a few hundredths, to well under a pixel). stamps and frames are 32-bit float data (serials and ids counted to 2^20, exact; times on the wrapped clock). scan is 8-bit. slit is 16-bit float: the film is resampled every frame for as long as it runs, and 8 bits would round it a little more each time. lib optical_flow()'s passes keep their own formats; pace and stamp are 32-bit, 1 x 1 and 2 x 1.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults: GPU 3.73, 3.72, 3.95 ms (95th percentile 4.48, 4.24, 4.20), CPU 0.73, 0.56, 0.50 ms (0.87, 0.76, 0.57), 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 3.7 ms (4.2) and 0.56 ms (0.76). Costliest: lk4 about 1.5 ms, lk8 0.7, scan 0.6, frames 0.3, flows 0.2. An earlier build measured 2.9 ms GPU with lk4 at 1.1 ms, so the flow's cost moves with the Mac's state. GPU memory measured with dev/probes/slit_memory.py for every History Size at 1280 x 720 and 1280 x 1280, History 1, 2 and 8 s (the page's table).
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new slit_scan.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Golan Levin's catalogue (linked), Fels and Mase's video cubism (NPIVM 1999, checked in the references of the 2000 paper) and Fels, Lee and Mase 2000 (linked), strip photography and Reinhart's tx-transform (1998), flow-based frame interpolation as a widely known technique with the library's Lucas-Kanade flow (Lucas and Kanade 1981) and Catmull-Rom resampling, lowbias32 (public domain, credited in lib/glsl/common.glsl where it sits), what the survey found and didn't use (td-slitscan, instant infinite's project file, elekktronaut's tutorial, the Time Machine TOP and its OP Snippet, linked), the beat clock (Dixon 2001, followed), WCAG 2.3.1, the preview footage and the depth model. The stamps, the frame list, the maps, the film, the tears and the limits are written for this library. A scan of the shaders found only lowbias32, value noise's smooth interpolation and Catmull-Rom (lib).
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second, 600 frames each. Worst second, area rule, Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone): dancer 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5 (1.0); 140 BPM 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5; no beat 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0; plaza 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.0 (0.5); flowers 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5 (1.0); koi 0.5 / 1.5 / 1.5 (0.5); Beat Kick 1 on the dancer 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5, flowers 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0, koi 0.5 / 1.5 / 1.5; Slit with Beat Kick 1 on the dancer 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0; Slit at Speed 120 (its cap) with Beat Kick 1 on the flowers 1.5 / 1.0 / 1.0 and koi 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.5; flowers with Liquid at Drift 1, Delay and History 8, Beat Kick 1 1.0 / 2.0 / 1.5; flowers with Spin 90, Delay and History 8, Beat Kick 1 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5; dancer with 32 Bands, Steps and Beat Kick 1 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5; plaza with Rings, 8 Bands, Delay and History 8, Beat Kick 1 0.5 / 0.5 / 0.5. Red at most 1.0 at Off, 2.5 at Calm, 2.0 at Up to 3, following the flowers' and koi's own; none in Slit. All within flashcount's bars. Earlier builds failed and were changed (board #4685): a beat ripple of extra delay (red 3.0 and 4.0 at Calm on flowers and koi, 3.0 at Beat Kick 1 on the dancer) and Slit's film surging on the beat (3.0 at Calm), replaced by tears; Drift 2 at Delay 1.5 (Off 2.0 on the dancer) and Drift 1 or Spin 90 at Delay 8 (Off 2.0 on the plaza, 2.5 and 2.0 on the flowers, red 3.5 and 3.0), so Drift and Spin now scale down with the delay, the delay glides at 1 s a second, and a flip's slide lengthens; Slit at Speed 160 and up (Off 2.0 to 4.0 on the flowers, 2.0 on the dancer from 250) and red at 120 (2.0, still 2.0 with only the laid film guarded), so Speed stops at 120 and every pixel Slit shows is guarded.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the effect re-times the picture and draws nothing of its own, and no text. Slit's red guard keeps very saturated reds short of WCAG's saturated-red line on every pixel it shows, so an orange there comes out a little lighter.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page. The docs page's Experimental section also says Flow can't see what a moving edge uncovers.
