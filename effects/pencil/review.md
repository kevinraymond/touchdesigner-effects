# pencil: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Style, Colors and Depth Type (discrete choices); Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Floats for Lines, Line Length, Shading, Stroke Size, Follow Movement, Texture, Depth Fade, Max Distance, Beat Kick, Loudness and Length; an Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Line Length, Stroke Size, Follow Movement, Depth Fade, Depth Type, Max Distance, Beat Kick, Song Moments). The longest names are Linelength and Strokesize (10 each), and the build passes the save-time name check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: classify's and shape's segment length (Line Length, and Song Moments' build and drop) and turn (Beat Kick, with the beat count); histogram's turn-over (Colors, Light on Dark) and tone's weights (Shading); draw's Style, Colors, Texture, Stroke Size, Lines, Loudness, Depth Fade (only with input 3 wired), Beat Kick's redraw and Follow Movement; drift's Follow Movement; orient's index (Style) and drifted's (Follow Movement, Style), so the tensor and the optical flow cook only when they are drawn; near's Depth Type and Max Distance; drop_env's hold (Length). The only script is parexec (Open Window, Open Docs): actions, not copied values. The model's constants (the segment's share of the height, the samples per segment, the line gain, the tone model's weightings, the cap, the stroke texture's sizes, the easing times, the redraw's turn and jump) live in the builder, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Style and Colors (the drawing), Lines and Line Length (the lines), Shading to Texture (the tone and the strokes), Depth Fade to Max Distance (input 3), Beat Kick to Length (the music), then the window. Shading, Stroke Size and Follow Movement gray out for Lines (no tone, no strokes), Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters, Length unless Song Moments is on.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count, a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step; beat_now feeds beat_n, a Count CHOP whose count picks each redraw's turn and jump, and drop_now starts drop_env (a Trigger CHOP: up 0.3 s, held for Length, down 0.8 s). build is read as a level, and level through level_smooth (0.5 s). Nothing reads the one-frame beat channel. With nothing wired no step comes, and the pressure term is 0 (it needs a source).
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  edges, tone and tensor_ease ease by 1 - exp(-step / time) with the frame step (absTime.stepSeconds, capped at 0.1 s), so their times (0.2 s, 1 s, 0.5 s) are seconds at any frame rate. drift carries the strokes by the motion (lib flow_speed: cells per second, held between the input's frames) times the frame step, and its two offsets restart by their age in seconds (absTime.seconds wrapped every CLOCK seconds, divided by the 1 s period), as does draw's fade between them. A drop's long lines and the loudness follow Trigger and Filter CHOPs in seconds. The redraws follow the beats themselves.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 369 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 62 and 65. orient (a Switch TOP) passes the structure tensor only for Hatching and drifted the optical flow only while Follow Movement is above 0 (and not for Lines), so neither chain cooks otherwise. strokes has no input and no changing uniform, so it cooks once. No expression reads another operator's size: small follows the picture through its Output Resolution menu (half), and the stateful passes read their feedback by UV.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  draw's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). edges and classify are 16-bit float RG (a gradient eased by small steps beside its set flag; a class vector), shape mono 16-bit float (a sum of gradients). histogram and tone are 256 x 1 32-bit float tables (shares near 1/256, and a table eased by small steps). strokes is 512 x 512 16-bit float RGBA in two buffers (six levels of hatching and Sketch's pattern, as transmittance; their mipmap means set Hatching's levels and Sketch's beta). drift is 32-bit float RGBA (two offsets in UV, which grow by a fraction of a pixel a frame and would round away at 16 bits). The tensor and the optical flow are lib's (16- and 32-bit float). near is lib's 16-bit float. No CHOP-to-TOP data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults (Sketch, Follow Movement 1), stress beat: GPU 5.20, 5.25, 5.51 ms (95th percentile 6.04, 6.42, 7.03), CPU 0.55, 0.61, 0.67 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 5.3 ms (6.4) and 0.61 ms. Costliest: shape 1.22-1.33, draw 1.11-1.19, lk4 0.94-0.97, classify 0.89-0.94, lk8 0.45, histogram 0.25. One run each: Hatching 5.95 ms (draw 1.86), Follow Movement 0 4.00 ms. Earlier builds with the old stroke texture (otherwise the same) measured 6.48, 6.60, 6.59 and, with a costlier histogram, 5.73, 5.95, 6.14: the GPU's speed wanders between series (classify, a fixed amount of work, read 0.83, 1.25 and 0.90 ms in the three).
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new pencil.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Lu, Xu and Jia 2012 (project page and DOI) for the lines, the tone model and the pencil pattern, written from the paper (read in full for this build), with the three changes said (closed-form beta, directions over half a turn, the cap) and how its Table 1's weights were read (its figures label the layers); Praun, Hoppe, Webb and Finkelstein 2001 (DOI) for the tonal art maps; Pizer et al. 1987 and Zuiderveld 1994 for the capped histogram; Abramowitz and Stegun 7.1.26 for erf; Max and Becker 1995 and Neyret 2003 for the advected textures, with Lucas and Kanade 1981 for the library's optical flow (shared with Datamosh, Fluid and VHS + CRT); Kyprianidis, Kang and Doellner 2009, Kyprianidis and Doellner 2011 and Jaehne, Scharr and Koerkel 1999 for the structure tensor (lib, shared with Paint and Reaction-Diffusion); Perlin 2002 for the paper's grain (lib paper.glsl, moved from Paint unchanged); lowbias32 (public domain, linked, copied into strokes.glsl with its credit, as in lib common.glsl); the depth cameras and lib depth reading; Dixon 2001 for the beat clock, followed; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Each shader's header names its source. The stroke texture drawn by a shader, the eight directions read whole and mixed, the easing of the edges and the tones, the redraw and the depth fade are written for this library and not called original. No TouchDesigner version of the method was found (a web search and the round-3 survey); nothing was copied or recalled from another implementation.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build (re-run in full after the stroke texture changed: every row as before), the demo paused, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second unless said, worst second by the area rule, Pencil (clip alone); no Flashing setting, so one recording each: dancer 1.0 (1.0); 140 BPM 1.0; no beat 1.0; plaza 1.0 (0.5); flowers 1.0 (1.0, red 1.0); koi 1.5 (0.5, red 1.5); Hatching dancer 1.5, 140 BPM 1.0, no beat 1.0, Beat Kick 0 1.0, flowers 1.0, plaza 1.0; Lines dancer 1.0; Light on Dark dancer 1.0; Colored Pencil flowers 1.0, red 1.0; Beat Kick 1 + Loudness 1 + Line Length 2 dancer 1.0; Follow Movement 0 dancer 1.0. Each within flashcount's promise for adding nothing (the worse of general and red, against the clip, with its half-flash margin): plaza +0.5, Hatching under 8 redraws a second +0.5, koi 1.5 against the clip's red 1.5. Found and fixed on the way (board #4707): the lines flickering with the clip's noise (1.5 against 1.0 with no beat; a wired loud input pressing the lines 15% harder tipped it over again at 0.1 s), so the gradient eases 0.2 s; the tone stretching a narrow band of tones over every tone, so the histogram is capped (the koi's worst cell 4.0 -> 3.0). What remains on the koi and the plaza is the clip's own change at a drawing's contrast (the near-black pond drawn mid-gray, the shadows as dense cross-hatching), checked by sampling water cells in the input and output together (dev/protos/pencil/water.py). Ruled out by measurement: Follow Movement, the drops, the tone's easing (1 s and 3 s) and a softer histogram. Red: the drawn colors make none; Colored Pencil keeps the picture's own.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color. Graphite is dark gray on warm white, Blue Pencil blue on warm white, Light on Dark near-white on near-black, and Colored Pencil keeps the picture's own colors with the drawing as their brightness; every one reads by brightness alone. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page. Follow Movement curving the strokes a little where the motion is noisy (smoke, ripples) is said plainly on the docs page (a limit of the flow, with Follow Movement lower as the remedy).
