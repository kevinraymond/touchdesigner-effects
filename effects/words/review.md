# words: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Layout, Case, Shape From, Depth Type, Color and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one); Toggles for Skip Common Words, Bold and Full Screen (states); a DAT parameter for Text (an operator, so it takes a dragged DAT); a Str for Font (any installed font, by name, as ASCII's Font Name); Floats for Largest, Smallest, Detail, Sensitivity, Max Distance, Spread, Follow, Fade, Fill Speed, Lift, Backdrop, Beat Pump and Swell; an Int for Display; Pulses for Reset, Open Window and Open Docs (actions). Labels in Title Case (Skip Common Words, Shape From, Fill Speed, Beat Pump). Skip Common Words' name is Skipcommon, Shape From's Shapefrom, Fill Speed's Fill, Beat Pump's Pump; the longest names are Skipcommon and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: text_in's DAT is Text; measure's and atlas's font and typeface follow Font and Bold; the words and order Script DATs read Case, Skip Common Words, Font, Bold and Layout in their callbacks, so they cook again when those change (a Script DAT does, 2025.33230); near reads Depth Type and Max Distance, find Sensitivity, subject and cells Shape From (Brightness when nothing is on input 3); moving passes the motion only for Cloud with Follow above 0 or Shape From Movement; field's slowing follows Smallest; layout reads Layout, Smallest, Largest, Detail, Spread, Follow, Fade and Fill Speed; word_mat reads Color, Lift and the beat's pump and the swell (only with input 2 wired); look reads Backdrop; the flash gate's spacing follows Flashing (lib flash_gate()). The scripts are reset (a Parameter Execute DAT: Reset pulses layout_fb's Reset) and parexec (Open Window, Open Docs); the two Script DATs compute the words from the text, they copy no parameter values. The method's constants (the slots, the levels, HOLD, the region's ease, the lines' slowing, the cell size past 1280, the room around words, the push, the pump and the swell) live in the builder and the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Layout to Bold (the layout and the words), Largest to Detail (sizes), Shape From to Spread (the subject), Follow to Reset (how words move, come and go), Color to Backdrop (colors), Beat Pump and Swell (input 2), Flashing, then the window. Grayed out where they don't apply: Skip Common Words and Spread unless Cloud, Largest in Lines (one size), Detail unless Portrait, Shape From in Portrait (which has no subject), Depth Type and Max Distance unless Shape From is Depth or Mask (Max Distance only for Distance in Meters), Follow unless Cloud. Sensitivity grays out with Shape From in Portrait; it applies to every Shape From.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises, flash_gate() lets it through as often as Flashing allows, and pump (a Trigger CHOP) holds the words up, falling over 0.35 s. level selects level, level_now smooths it over 1 s (a Filter CHOP) and level_usual is its usual level (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s). The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, source is 0 and there is no pump and no swell.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize. The two Script DATs keep no state between cooks (each cook rebuilds its table from the text).
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The layout steps by the frame's step (absTime.stepSeconds, capped at 0.1 s): fades in Fade seconds (out in half), a word out of its region for HOLD (0.15 s) before it goes, words' ages in seconds, the idle wait after a search finds no room in seconds (0.25), Cloud's words carried by the motion in pixels a second times the step, and how many words come a frame from Fill Speed (words a second) times the step. The region eases by 1 - exp(-step / 0.15 s); Lines' lines move at most a line's spacing a second times the step. The pump's fall is a Trigger CHOP's decay in seconds (0.35), the gate's spacing a Trigger CHOP's re-trigger delay in seconds, the loudness's smoothing a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and its usual level one 4 s wide. Nothing counts frames (the order's place and Lines' line end carry over in the layout's state, not by frame).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 372 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 67 and 76. The text lane (words, order, measure, atlas, ink, vocab, order_tex) cooks only when the text, the font, the case or the layout changes. Portrait and Lines leave the optical flow resting (moving passes a 1 x 1 constant), so lk8 and its passes don't cook there.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  look's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie); render is 8-bit RGBA (the words, premultiplied); the atlas is the Text TOP's own 8-bit. Data textures in 32-bit float: vocab and order_tex (CHOP to TOPs of the word tables), ink (atlas pixel coordinates up to 1280), cells, region and field (distances in cells, slowed by small steps a frame), layout (positions in pixels, ages in seconds). seed and jfa are 16-bit float (cell coordinates up to 160, exact), rings 16-bit float (the slowed distance and its slope, read filtered).
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs per layout with /tdlib_demo paused (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat, dancer clip with its depth: Cloud GPU 2.36, 2.37, 2.41 ms (95th percentile 3.78, 3.80, 3.80), CPU 1.12, 1.11, 1.11 ms; Portrait GPU 1.79, 1.73, 1.82 ms (2.61, 2.58, 2.78), CPU 0.81, 0.81, 1.22 ms; Lines GPU 3.13, 2.99, 2.93 ms (4.55, 4.41, 4.30), CPU 1.07, 0.82, 0.82 ms; 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians. Costliest: layout (0.7 Cloud, 0.8 Portrait, 2.1 Lines), lk8 0.67 (Cloud only), render 0.5-0.65. Before: 4.5 ms for Cloud with the flow at a quarter size (lk4 1.5 ms) and the masks built with atomics; Lines 5.4 ms reading its field by hand and searching every frame. The README DAT says about 2.4 ms (Cloud), 1.8 ms (Portrait) and 3 ms (Lines).
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new words.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Wordle (Feinberg 2008; Viegas, Wattenberg and Feinberg, InfoVis 2009) for placing words; ShapeWordle (Wang et al., VIS 2019) for filling a shape by its distance field from the deepest point; EdWordle (Wang et al., VIS 2017) for keeping a layout and pushing words as rigid bodies; micrography and Maharik, Bessmeltsev, Sheffer, Shamir and Carr's Digital Micrography (SIGGRAPH 2011) for text along lines that follow a figure; Rong and Tan (I3D 2006) for jump flooding; Lucas and Kanade (1981) for the library's flow, and lib depth_find; Whitman's text (public domain, Project Gutenberg #1322, checked word for word against it); the font as the user's own; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Each source's header says the same where it sits (layout.glsl Wordle, ShapeWordle, EdWordle, micrography and Digital Micrography; jfa.glsl Rong and Tan; text's comment the poem's source). The sums and the best spot in shared memory are atomics, nothing taken from a source. Nothing is called original: the grid of bits, the atomic-maximum search, Portrait's sizes from detail, Lines' slowed level sets and written-on text, the keeping, carrying, pushing and fading, the ink finding, the pump through the gate and the swell are said to be written for this library. The docs page has a Patents paragraph (search 2026-10-28, private/patents/words.md: nothing from the papers' authors; two Adobe patents near it, US 11,610,350 and US 10,559,061, whose claims it stays clear of) and the README names them as Not used.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py, one run at a time, each Flashing setting, worst second by the area rule (red where any), Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone). Stress beat: dancer Cloud 0.5/0.5/0.5 (1.0); Portrait 0.5/1.0/3.0 (1.0); Lines 1.0/1.0/2.0 (1.0); hands Cloud 1.0/1.0/1.0 (1.5); flowers Cloud 1.0/1.0/3.0 r0.5 (1.0 r1.0). Dance beat (140 BPM): dancer Cloud 0.5/0/0.5 (1.0). Corners, spot-checked: Lines in white 1.0/1.0/1.0, Portrait in white 1.0/1.0/3.0, Beat Pump 1 0.5/0.5/1.0 (clip 1.0 each). Cloud's last change (a fading word keeps its cells) came after that table: the dancer was re-measured at every setting and the hands and flowers at Off on the final build (above); the other Cloud rows are from the build before it. What the measuring found and fixed on the way: Lines' rings followed the dancer at once and swept text and gap through cells about a line apart (Off 2.0 against the clip's 1.0), so the field moves at most a line's spacing a second (its first version snapped while the eased subject was still empty and stayed at no outline, leaving Lines with no words: caught by counting words, dev/probes/words_lines_dbg.py); white words riding the dancer through Portrait (Off 2.0) made Follow Cloud-only; a word between two older ones, pushed out of all its overlap each frame, shook every other frame (19 flashes a second in one cell, white Lines; dev/probes/words_frames.py) until the push became half; fading every word at a cut took the whole picture black at each clip loop (white Portrait, whole 1.0), so words stay through cuts.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the words take the picture's own colors (or white), and their sizes and places carry the picture. The text is the point: the default Smallest (18 px a line, letters about 13 px) is legible at 1280 x 720, Bold on; Lines writes at Smallest, the smallest legible size, and the docs say smaller fills more closely but reads less. Lift keeps dark words from vanishing into a dark picture, and in Cloud gives black words a floor of gray.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Nothing of its own is unreliable: the layout is deterministic for a given picture. Its limits are said plainly on the docs page (a greedy layout is never as tight as an optimized one; a cell is 8 pixels; the subject is only as good as what finds it). The beats and loudness come from audio_in(), whose experimental parts are labeled on their own pages. No Experimental section is needed.
