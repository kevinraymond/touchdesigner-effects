# voxel_space: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Flight, Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one); Floats for Speed, Height, Smooth, Light, Fog, Bass Lift, Beat Kick and Max Distance; an Int for Display; a Toggle for Full Screen (a state); Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Bass Lift, Beat Kick, Depth Type, Max Distance). Bass Lift's name is Basslift and Beat Kick's Kick; the longest names are Fullscreen and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: near reads Depth Type and Max Distance; settle eases less while dip_env is up; relief reads Flight (the island's shore in Orbit); smooth's size is Smooth x 20 pixels; terrain reads Height (times drive's lift) and Light; flight reads Flight, Speed (times pace), Height and drive's dip; render reads Height, Fog and Flight. drive holds the lift (Bass Lift times the low end's level and its rise over its usual level, 0 with nothing on input 2) and the dip (Beat Kick times dip_env); pace_ask turns tempo into a pace. Values only the inside needs (the world's size, the steps, the easing, the flights' paths) are constants in the builder and the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Flight, Speed, Height, Smooth, Light and Fog (the flight and the land); Bass Lift and Beat Kick (the music); Depth Type and Max Distance (input 3); Flashing; then the window. Max Distance grays out unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. Every other parameter applies to all three flights (Speed moves each camera its own way), so nothing else grays out; Depth Type stays live with nothing on input 3, as other depth effects' does, its help saying the brightness stands in.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). lows selects sub_bass and bass, lows_mean averages them, lows_now smooths them over 1 s (a Filter CHOP) and lows_usual is their usual level (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s). counts selects beat_count and steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises: flash_gate() passes it as Flashing allows and dip_env (a Trigger CHOP) shapes the dip; tempo (a box Filter CHOP over 6 s) averages steps into beats a second for pace_ask. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, source is 0: the lift is 0, no beats come, and the pace is 1.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The camera moves by its pace times the frame's step (absTime.stepSeconds, at most 0.1 s), the heights, the colors, the view and the span ease by exp(-step / time), and Fly's height eases the same way, so a dropped frame moves them as far as the frames it stood for. Fly's path is a function of the distance flown, Orbit's and Hover's of their phases, all carried in flight's own feedback. The dip is a Trigger CHOP's attack and decay in seconds, the gate's spacing a re-trigger delay in seconds, the low end's smoothing a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and its usual level 4 s, the tempo counted over 6 s and the pace eased over 2 s.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 258 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 45 and 49. The feedback loops (span, ease, colors, flight, settle) run only while something pulls the output, the compute shader dispatches a fixed 1280 workgroups and returns past the picture's width, and flight reads the picture's shape from its own input, so no expression reads another operator's size.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  colors and settle are 16-bit float: eased in 8 bits, a step under 1/255 of the way rounds to nothing and the colors stopped short. depth_guess and depth_soft (the brightness at a quarter size) take the input's format. near, relief, ease and terrain are 16-bit float (heights 0-1, and the light); rows, span and flight are 32-bit float data (the rows' baselines and reaches, the camera's position in world units up to 16 picture heights, its height and heading). render takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). mean is a 32-bit Analyze TOP (one pixel). drive's and pace's channels feed uniforms, not a data texture.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, the dancer's depth on input 3, stress beat: GPU 2.19, 2.18, 2.17 ms (95th percentile 2.39, 2.42, 2.39), CPU 0.48, 0.49, 0.50 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 2.2 ms (2.4) and 0.49 ms. Costliest: render about 0.4 ms (1280 workgroups of 512 steps), smooth 0.34 (the Blur TOP at half size), terrain 0.30, colors 0.29, relief 0.23. Measured on the build before the last text-only rebuilds (help, README), which change no operator.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new voxel_space.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Kyle Freeman and NovaLogic's Voxel Space in Comanche (1992; US 5,550,959 and 6,020,893, expired); Sebastian Macke's s-macke/VoxelSpace (code MIT), followed for the method with none of its code and none of Comanche's maps; Hillis and Steele's scan (CACM 1986) for the running minimum; the library's depth_near.glsl; the library's red guard (shared with VHS + CRT, Bent Cam, Motion Magnify, Slit-Scan and Scan Processor); Fosfora's seven-band split; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). render.glsl names Freeman, NovaLogic, the patents, Macke's write-up and Hillis and Steele where the method sits. The docs page names the usual TouchDesigner ways to make a terrain from a picture (displaced geometry, RayTK and forum raymarchers) and the patents searched (Evans and Sutherland's expired US 5,579,456, Sony's active US 10,198,848 and why it doesn't apply). Original, after a search: each row's heights from its own far baseline, the scale from the tallest row, the land easing as one, a workgroup a column with a binary search per pixel, the colors between steps and cliff faces in their tops' colors, the flights and the music's lift, dip and pace.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final behavior (the dip down over 0.25 s, settle easing less during it, Fly along the seam at 28 units a second), one run at a time, each Flashing setting, worst second by the area rule, red where any: Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone). Stress beat, Fly: dancer 1.0/1.5/1.5 (1.0); walkers 1.0/1.0/1.5 (1.0); flowers 1.0/1.5/2.0, red 0 (1.0 r1.0); koi 1.0/1.0/1.5, red 0 (0.5 r1.5); plaza 1.0/1.5/1.0 (0.5). Orbit: dancer 0.5/1.0/1.0, plaza 0.5/1.0/1.0. Hover: dancer 1.0/1.5/1.5, plaza 1.0/1.0/1.0. Dancer, 140 BPM 1.0/1.0/1.5; a song 1.0/1.0/1.5; no music 1.5/1.0/1.0; Beat Kick 1 1.0/1.5/1.5. Corners at Off: Speed 2 dancer 1.0, plaza 1.0; Height 2 dancer 1.5. Every row within its rule; the plaza at Off and the dancer with no music at the half-flash margin. On the way (board #4746): a 3D view enlarges the near ground, so the clip's own changes cover more of the view (the picture flat, camera still: 2.0 vs 1.0); cliff faces in their tops' colors, land and colors eased together over 0.25 s, brightness softened, the red guard (flowers 3.5 red, koi 5.5 red before it), the view eased 0.1 s, and Fly slowed from 70 to 45 to 28 brought every row in.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the land keeps the picture's own colors (through the red guard), its shape read by light and shade (a low sun from the left) and by the fog. The sky and the sea take the picture's mean color. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Nothing here is unreliable enough to mark experimental: the lift reads only the low bands, which every source gives (Fosfora's bass stands in for both without its feature bus, a fair stand-in for the low end), and the tempo is the beats counted over 6 s. The docs page says plainly that the row baselines suit scenes with a floor and that a scene without one (a room close up) comes out in soft terraces.
