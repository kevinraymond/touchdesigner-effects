# lens_flare: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Lens, Coating, Show and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one); Ints for Blades, Lights and Display; Floats for Aperture, Roundness, Blade Angle, Amount, Starburst, Starburst Size, Threshold, Beat Kick and Swell; a Toggle for Full Screen (a state); Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs (actions). Labels in Title Case (Blade Angle, Starburst Size, Beat Kick). Blade Angle's name is Angle, Starburst Size's Burstsize, Beat Kick's Kick; the longest names are Burstsize and Openwindow (9 and 10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: bright reads Threshold; lights reads Lights; ghost_table's index is Lens's menu index; aperture reads Blades, Blade Angle and Roundness, and ghost_mat the same with Aperture, Coating's index and the ghosts' gain (Amount times the beat's kick and the swell, only with input 2 wired); burst_mat reads Starburst (with the same kick and swell) and Starburst Size; ghost_geo's and burst_geo's instance counts follow Lights (times the lens's ghosts, ghost_tex's width); look reads Show; the flash gate's spacing follows Flashing (lib flash_gate()). The only script is parexec (Open Window, Open Docs). The method's constants (the tiles, MERGE, the energy's compression S, the easing, VEIL, the faintest ghost, the starburst's zoom and tone, the gain, the kick and the swell) live in the builder and the shaders, and the lenses' ghosts in their tables, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Lens to Aperture (the lens), Blades to Blade Angle (the iris), Amount to Starburst Size (how strong), Threshold and Lights (the lights), Show, Beat Kick and Swell (input 2), Flashing, then the window. Every parameter applies in every mode (Starburst Size at Starburst 0 shows nothing, as Amount 0 shows no ghosts), so nothing grays out.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises, flash_gate() lets it through as often as Flashing allows, and kick (a Trigger CHOP) holds the flare up, falling over 0.4 s. level selects level, level_now smooths it over 1 s (a Filter CHOP) and level_usual is its usual level (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s). The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, source is 0 and the gain is Amount alone.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The tiles ease by 1 - exp(-step / 0.2 s) and the flare by 1 - exp(-step / 0.1 s), with the frame step (absTime.stepSeconds, capped at 0.1 s); the kick's fall is a Trigger CHOP's decay in seconds (0.4), the gate's spacing a Trigger CHOP's re-trigger delay in seconds, the loudness's smoothing a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and its usual level one 4 s wide. Nothing counts frames, and nothing reads the clock (the flare is a function of the picture's lights).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 285 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 41 and 50. The starburst lane (aperture, dft_rows, dft, burst) and the ghost table cook once, and again only when the iris or the lens changes (dev/probes/flare_cooks.py: 0 cooks in 120 frames while the output is pulled).
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  look's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). bright is 16-bit float RGBA at a quarter size (linear colors times a weight). tiles and lights are 32-bit float RGBA data textures (sums of positions and energies in picture units squared, down to 1e-9), ghost_tex is a 32-bit float data texture from a CHOP to TOP (the ghost table), and the starburst's transform (dft_rows, dft) is 32-bit float (sums of 256 terms, then the power over the open area squared). render, flare and burst are 16-bit float RGBA (linear light above 1 near the lights, added up).
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat, dancer clip: GPU 1.46, 1.43, 1.40 ms (95th percentile 2.09, 1.77, 1.72), CPU 0.60, 0.56, 0.50 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 1.4 ms (1.8) and 0.6 ms. Costliest: render 1.1 (the ghosts), tiles 0.24, lights 0.2. Night field (five lanterns) 1.66 ms, headlights 3.18 ms (render 2.8). Before: render 7.0 ms at full size with 4x antialiasing, 4.4 at half size (the ghosts' fragments: Amount 0 took the render to 0.09 ms), then the three colors sharing one angle, faint ghosts culled (FAINTEST 0.001) and Lights 6 by default. The starburst's transform (dft_rows, dft, burst) about 1.7 ms once, when the iris changes (dev/probes/flare_cooks.py). The README DAT says about 1.4 ms.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new lens_flare.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Lee and Eisemann (EGSR 2013) for the method (paraxial ray transfer matrices per two-reflection ghost split at the iris, the iris's shape cut by the entrance pupil, a channel each at 650/510/475 nm, the area ratio); Hullin, Eisemann, Seidel and Lee (SIGGRAPH 2011) for thin-film coatings and the starburst over wavelengths with dust; the four lenses' patents (Tronnier US 2,645,156; Bertele US 1,975,678; Richter US 1,792,917; Isono US 6,744,570) and Nathan Hagen's LensLibrary (MIT, its notice kept in src/lenses.py) for their prescriptions; Macleod for characteristic matrices and Thelen for the quarter-half-quarter coating; Abbe and Cauchy for the glass's dispersion; Wyman, Sloan and Shirley (JCGT 2013) for the CIE 1931 fit (checked against the colorscience R package at 555 nm); Hillis and Steele (CACM 1986) for the parallel reduction; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Each source's header says the same where it sits (optics.py the method and the coatings, lenses.py the patents and LensLibrary's notice, ghost_vertex the method, burst the starburst and the color fit, lights the reduction). The sRGB conversions are the standard formulas. Nothing is called original: the light finding, the blur by the light's spread, the coatings' normalization, VEIL, the two-pass transform, the easing, the kick through the gate and the swell are said to be written for this library. The docs page has a Patents paragraph (search 2026-10-28, private/patents/lens_flare.md: the method's own patent, US 9,595,132, lapsed in 2025; Hullin et al.'s application abandoned) and names as Not used the precomputed table of non-linear ghost shapes that Sungkyunkwan's continuation US 10,074,195 claims (the paper's Section 5 experiment).
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build, one run at a time, each Flashing setting, worst second by the area rule (red where any), Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone). Stress beat: dancer 1.0/1.0/1.0 (1.0); headlights 1.0/1.5/1.0 (1.0); night field 0.5/0.5/0.5 (0); flowers 1.0 r1.0 each (1.0 r1.0). Dance beat (140 BPM): dancer 1.0 each (1.0). Corners, spot-checked: headlights at Beat Kick 1 1.0/1.5/1.0 (1.0); flowers with the phone camera multicoated 1.0 r1.0 each (1.0 r1.0). What the measuring found and fixed on the way: the lights jumped when another tile became the brightest and a tile switched lights, and the ghosts, at up to 2.4 times the light's offset, swept over large areas (headlights Off 2.5 against the clip's 1.0, Up to 3 per second 4.0, FAIL): tiles now join a light with a weight that fades with distance, and the flare is eased over 0.1 s. Lanterns bobbing with the walkers' steps swept their hot ghosts back and forth (night field Off 1.0 against the clip's 0): the tiles are eased over 0.2 s (Off 0.5, the margin). The beat's kick, 1.75 times at the default, rises through the flare's easing: dev/probes/flare_kick.py shows the flare doubling at Beat Kick 1 once a second at Calm.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the flare is light added where the picture's lights would put it, brighter where they are brighter, moving as they move. The coatings' tints are a look, not a signal. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Nothing of its own is unreliable: the ghosts are worked out from the lenses' designs, and the lights from the picture's brightness. Its limits are said plainly on the docs page (first-order optics: no ghost bends as a real one does far from the axis; a light's brightness above white is unknown, so its energy is compressed). The beats and loudness come from audio_in(), whose experimental parts are labeled on their own pages. No Experimental section is needed.
