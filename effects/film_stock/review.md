# film_stock: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Stock, Gauge, Frame Rate and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Exposure, Contrast, Saturation, Grain, Halation, Weave, Flicker, Dirt, Day for Night, Beat Kick and Loudness; a Toggle for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); an Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Day for Night, Beat Kick, Frame Rate, Song Moments). Day for Night's name is Night, Frame Rate's Framerate, Beat Kick's Kick and Song Moments' Moments; the longest names are Saturation, Fullscreen and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: film_frame reads Frame Rate; cadence's index Frame Rate; gate's uniforms Weave, Beat Kick (times kick_env, off when Flashing is Off), Song Moments (the splice, with drop_kind and splice_env) and Exposure; night's Day for Night and scene's index the same; halo's size Gauge; negative's uniforms Stock, Halation, Grain (times the loudness push through level_smooth, only when input 2 is wired) and Gauge; print's Stock, Contrast and Saturation; show's Flicker, Dirt and Song Moments (the cue); beat_on's gain Beat Kick, drop_on's Song Moments; the gate and budget Flashing. No script copies values. The stocks' tables (curves, grain radii and spreads, resolving powers, halation, dyes, tints), the gauges' frame heights and halo sizes, the weave's and jitter's micrometers, the kick's and splice's sizes and fades, the cue mark's time, the switch radius, the scanner's sigma, the flash spacing and the flicker's cap stay in the builder and shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Stock to Saturation (the film and its exposure), Grain to Frame Rate (what the film and the print add), Day for Night, Beat Kick to Song Moments (the music), then Flashing and the window. Nothing is grayed out: every parameter applies in every mode (Halation and Weave scale with Gauge, Grain with Stock), and Beat Kick, Loudness and Song Moments act only with something on input 2, which their help says, as no library effect grays a parameter by its wiring.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, drop_count and section_count; steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame each rises. beat_on and drop_on ask for kicks and splices from the beat and drop steps, flash_limit and flash (lib flash_gate()) and flash_budget ration them, kick_env and splice_env shape them (Trigger CHOPs), drop_kind (a Hold CHOP of drop_env at the budget's pulse) says whether the event that passed was a drop's; cue (a Trigger CHOP) shows the cue mark on section steps; level_pick and level_smooth (a Filter CHOP, 1 s) are the loudness for the grain's push. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired there are no kicks and the push is 1.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The weave, the flicker's breathing and the scratches' lives follow lib clock_seconds() (absTime.seconds wrapped every 16384 s); a kick settles over 0.3 s and a splice over 0.2 s (Trigger CHOPs in seconds), the cue mark shows for 0.17 s, and the loudness is smoothed over a second. The film's frame number is int(absTime.seconds x the frame rate) in Python (double precision), so a dropped frame drops a film frame rather than slowing the film; at Frame Rate Source it is absTime.frame, since then there is nothing to time, only something to seed. Nothing else counts frames.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 349 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 38 and 41. hold_fb starts from a 1 x 1 constant and hold takes its size from the source through its input, so no expression reads another operator's size; cadence and scene are Switch TOPs, so the hold and the night chain cook only when chosen.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  hold is 16-bit float (the frame's number in its alpha, exact to 2048); gate, night_src, night, glare and the halo are 16-bit float (linear light, above 1 at the halo); negative is 16-bit float (each layer's transmittance, which the print reads as a log); print is 16-bit float (linear light); show is 8-bit, the picture. still is 1 x 1 32-bit. The Blur TOPs take their inputs' formats. No data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults (Color Negative 500T, 16 mm), stress beat: GPU 1.91, 2.32, 2.29 ms (95th percentile 3.12, 2.98, 2.75), CPU 0.39, 0.40, 0.39 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives 2.3 ms (3.0) and 0.4 ms. Costliest: print 0.8-1.0 (its two 7 x 7 reads of the negative), negative 0.6-0.75 (the grain), show 0.2, glare 0.2. Corners, one run each: Super 8 at Grain 3 (the grains drawn one by one) 500T 5.9 ms GPU, Black and White 400 6.8, Vintage 13.4, all at 60 fps; an earlier build enumerated five times the grains it needed and took 17-28 ms there (dev/probes/film_stock_cost.py found the loop itself, not the lens or the hashing, was the cost). Day for Night 3.64 ms with its blurs at half size (4.90 at full size).
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new film_stock.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name the Boolean model (Matheron 1967, Serra 1982) as Newson, Delon and Galerne used it for film grain (CGF 2017; with Faraj, IPOL 2017), followed, not copied (the count or the grains drawn, not their Monte Carlo sample); Nutting's formula (1913) with Kodak's published rms granularity and resolving power (data sheets F-4017, F-4016, H-1-5219), the names used only to say which figures; Hurter and Driffield's characteristic curve (1890); Thompson, Shirley and Ferwerda's night filtering (JGT 2002) with Larson, Rushmeier and Piatko's scotopic luminance (1997), the second blur a triangle; the SMPTE frame heights; lowbias32 (public domain, credited in lib/glsl/common.glsl where it sits), the library's Catmull-Rom and red guard; WCAG 2.3.1; the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Not used, with the patent numbers and why: Zhang, Wang, Tian and Pappas's statistics-based rendering (Northwestern US 12,705,810), AV1's scaled noise (Netflix US 10,839,489), grain measured from a reference image (Autodesk US 7,873,210), summed Gaussians (NVIDIA US 8,064,726). The shaders name the methods where they sit. Original, after a search: the stocks' tables and tints, the halation knee, the gate's weave and kicks, the splice, the dirt and scratches, the print's color read softer than its brightness.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the 0.1.0 build, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second, 600 frames each. Worst second, area rule, Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone): dancer 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0 (1.0); flowers 0.5 / 0.5 / 1.5 (1.0); koi at Off 0.5 (0.5). Red 0 everywhere (the flowers alone 1.0, the koi 1.5). All within flashcount's bars: Off adds nothing, Calm at most one, and the kicks and splices move the picture less than a cell of the test's grid. The flicker is capped at 5% of the light, under WCAG's 10%, so it can't count.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the stocks differ in grain, softness and contrast as well as in tint, halation is a glow around bright things (brighter as well as redder), and the kicks, splices and cue mark read by movement and shape. No text is drawn. The red guard keeps halation's reds short of WCAG's saturated-red line.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page.
