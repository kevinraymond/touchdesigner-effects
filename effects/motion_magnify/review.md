# motion_magnify: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Toggles for Follow Tempo, Song Moments and Full Screen (states); a Menu for Depth Type (discrete choices); Floats for Amount, Timing, Smoothness, Depth Focus, Max Distance, Beat Kick, Loudness and Length; an Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Follow Tempo, Depth Focus, Depth Type, Max Distance, Beat Kick, Song Moments). Follow Tempo's name is Tempo, since Followtempo is 11 characters (the save check refused it); the longest names are Smoothness and Depthfocus (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: move's leak and easing times (Timing, or half the beat rate from beat_rate with Follow Tempo while beats come at 60 BPM or more); spread_h's and spread_v's sigma (Smoothness); stretch's amount (Amount, Loudness through level_smooth while input 2 has a source, Beat Kick through kick_env, Song Moments' build and drop_env) and Depth Focus (only with input 3 wired); moved's index (Amount above 0), so the optical flow doesn't cook at 0; near's Depth Type and Max Distance; drop_env's hold (Length). The only script is parexec (Open Window, Open Docs): actions, not copied values. The model's constants (the band's corners at half and twice Timing, the spread at 12 px, the fold guard's 0.6 pixels per pixel and 12 px reach, the flow's dead zone, the music's gains, the tempo's 4 s) live in the builder, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Amount to Smoothness (the movement), Depth Focus to Max Distance (input 3), Beat Kick to Length (the music), then the window. Max Distance grays out unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters, Length unless Song Moments is on. Follow Tempo stays live without input 2 (its help says it needs beats, and it falls back to Timing without them).
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count, and a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step; beat_now starts kick_env (a Trigger CHOP, up at once, back over 0.4 s) and feeds beat_rate (a box Filter over 4 s: the beats a second, for Follow Tempo); drop_now starts drop_env (up 0.3 s, held for Length, down 0.8 s). build is read as a level, and level through level_smooth (0.5 s). Nothing reads the one-frame beat channel. With nothing wired no step comes, beat_rate stays 0 (Timing is used), and the loudness term is 0 (it needs a source).
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  move carries and adds the motion (lib flow_speed: cells per second, held between the input's frames) times the frame step (absTime.stepSeconds, capped at 0.1 s), and leaks and eases by exp(-step / time), so the leak's and the easing's times (from Timing in Hz) are seconds at any frame rate and a 16 fps clip moves as a live camera would. The pushes, a drop's larger movement, the loudness and the tempo follow Trigger and Filter CHOPs in seconds.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 269 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 52 and 55. moved (a Switch TOP) passes the displacement only while Amount is above 0, so the optical flow and the displacement don't cook at 0. No expression reads another operator's size: the stateful passes take their size from the motion and read their feedback by UV.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  warp's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). move is 32-bit float RGBA (a displacement in UV units that grows by a fraction of a pixel a frame and leaks by small steps, which would round away at 16 bits), spread_h and spread_v 32-bit float RG, stretch and guard 32-bit float RGBA (the displacement, its stretch and how much is kept). The optical flow and its speed are lib's (32-bit float), near lib's 16-bit float. No CHOP-to-TOP data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat: GPU 3.54, 3.57, 3.67 ms (95th percentile 4.94, 5.03, 4.98), CPU 0.52, 0.52, 0.51 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 3.6 ms (5.0) and 0.5 ms. Costliest: lk4 1.55-1.62, lk8 0.85, guard 0.42-0.44. Amount 0: 0.07 ms. Three earlier runs with the demo running another effect (the flash gate still in, otherwise the same) measured 2.20, 2.47, 3.06 ms: the GPU's speed wanders with its load, as BUILDING.md says, and the page says so.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new motion_magnify.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Liu, Torralba, Freeman, Durand and Adelson 2005 (project page) for Lagrangian motion magnification, followed, not copied; Flotho, Heiss, Steidl and Strauss 2023 (DOI) as the nearest later dense-flow method, not built after; Stam 1999 (DOI) for semi-Lagrangian advection, as in Pencil; Choi and Lee 2000 (DOI) for the injectivity condition behind the fold guard; Lucas and Kanade 1981 for the library's optical flow; the red guard (lib, shared with Bent Cam, Slit-Scan and VHS + CRT); the depth cameras and lib depth reading; Dixon 2001 for the beat clock, followed; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). They also name what is not used and why: Wu et al. 2012 and Wadhwa et al. 2013 and 2014, patented in the US by MIT until 2032-2035 (patent numbers given; private/patents/motion-magnify.md holds the claims read, board #4708, #4709), with the note that the MIT license covers the code, not anyone's patents. Called original (no earlier source found) after two web searches for streaming, flow-integrated magnification and one for fold-free warps (2026-10-26): the displacement added up frame by frame, carried with the content and leaking back to rest, and the fold guard's local holding back. Follow Tempo, the pushes and the depth focus are written for this library and not called original. Each shader's header says what it does and after whom; nothing was copied or recalled from another implementation.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build (no Flashing setting, so one recording each), stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second unless said, worst second by the area rule, Motion Magnify (clip alone): dancer 1.0 (1.0); 140 BPM 1.0; no beat 1.0; Beat Kick 1 1.0; night field 0.5 (0); plaza 0.5 (0.5); flowers 1.5 (1.0, red 1.0); flowers Beat Kick 1 1.5; koi 1.0 (0.5, red 1.5); portrait 1.5 (0.5), 140 BPM 1.0, no beat 1.0; corner Amount 10 + Beat Kick 1 + Loudness 1 on the flowers 2.0 (1.0), spot-checked as 'not crazy' (board #4687). Red is 0 in every row. Found and fixed on the way (board #4710): swaying the red flowers wider swept reds near WCAG's saturated-red line across it (red 2.0 with no beat, the clip 1.0), so warp.glsl runs lib red_guard.glsl on every pixel. With a flash gate the beat's pushes measured the same as Flashing Off on every clip, and ungated at 8 a second they added nothing on the dancer and half a flash on the flowers and the portrait (none at 140 BPM), so the effect has no Flashing setting, as Pencil. What it adds is the clip's own change moved further (the portrait's smile, the flowers' sway), within WCAG's 3.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the output is the picture's own colors, moved, with saturated reds eased a little short of WCAG's red line. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page. Where the optical flow goes wrong (flat areas, smoke, reflections, one thing sliding in front of another) is said plainly on the docs page's Experimental section, and the background dragged near what moves on its Amount section.
