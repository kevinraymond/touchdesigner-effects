# point_cloud: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.4

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Look, Points From, Depth Type, Detail and Color (discrete choices); Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen; Floats for Stand Out, Far Limit, Max Distance, Depth, Size, Brightness, Turn, Tilt, Distance, Sway, Sway Time, Loudness, Beat Swell and Bar Moves, Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case; the longest names are Pointsfrom and Brightness (10), and the build passes the save-time check. 0.1.2 changes two defaults only (Stand Out 0.10, Far Limit 0.15, as in Particles); no parameter added or renamed.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the uniforms of near and near_small (Depth Type, Max Distance), find (Points From, Stand Out, Far Limit), pos (Depth, Depth Type, Look for the jitter, Detail for its size and the smoothing radius) and the three GLSL MATs (Size, Look, Color, Brightness, Depth, Turn, Tilt, Distance, Sway, Sway Time, Beat Swell, Loudness, Song Moments, Detail); dot_mat's blending and depth test (Look); the Switch TOPs keep (Points From) and look (Look); bar (Bar Moves) and bar_lag's glide (Look). No script copies a value; the only one is finish_effect's parexec (Open Window, Open Docs). The camera's field of view, the smoothing and edge thresholds, the splat size, the sway's reach and the music's strengths stay in the builder; the dice and the swirl in the shaders, off the page. 0.1.2: find's Far Limit uniform is an expression of the page, Far Limit while input 3 is wired, else the larger of Far Limit and BRIGHT_FAR (0.5, a builder constant).
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Look; Points From to Max Distance (what makes the cloud); Depth to Brightness (its shape and color); Turn to Sway Time (the camera); Loudness to Song Moments (the music); Open Window, Full Screen, Display. Stand Out is grayed out unless Points From is The Subject, Far Limit when Points From is Everything, Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters, and Size when Look is Surface (splats size themselves). Depth Type stays live: it also decides how depth turns into distance.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(); beats and drops come from beat_count and drop_count through a Slope CHOP (steps) into Trigger CHOPs, and the camera's angle per bar from bar_count. Nothing reads the one-frame beat. 0.1.1 changes only lib/audio_callbacks.py (#4652): raw audio's beats now come from a beat clock locked to the kicks' tempo (bars every 4 of its beats), and Fosfora's level from its bass. Its logic is unchanged here. Checked by dev/selftest.py (every input kind, Fosfora's level from bass) and dev/beat_bench.py --selftest.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension: the effect is operators and expressions. Its only state is in Feedback TOPs (the eased middle, pivot_fb) and CHOP envelopes, so nothing needs reinitializing.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The sway and the scatter follow absTime.seconds; the middle eases by absTime.stepSeconds (pivot.glsl, an exponential in seconds); the beat's swell, the drop's burst and the build fade in seconds (Trigger and Lag CHOPs in seconds), and the bar's glide is a Lag CHOP in seconds. 0.1.3: Dot_vertex.glsl's clock (the build's scatter wobble) is lib clock_seconds(), wrapped every 16384 s so a 32-bit uniform keeps it to 1 ms (board #4681); the wobble jumps once at each wrap, only while a build scatters the dots. The camera's sway is computed in Python (math.sin, double precision) and hands the shader its angle.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py: 0 of 86 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired to the clip, its depth and the stress beat; the control pulled 42 and 45. The camera moves nothing: the cloud turns in dot_mat's shader from uniforms, evaluated only when a render cooks. dev/idle.py on the 0.1.1 build: 0 of 86 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 42 and 45. dev/idle.py on the 0.1.2 build: 0 of 86 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 42 and 45. dev/idle.py on the 0.1.4 build: 0 of 269 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 42 and 45.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  32-bit float for positions and their averages (pos, pivot_in, pivot_avg, pivot, pivot_start); 16-bit float for nearness (near, near_small, near_tiny, near_pick, find and depth_stats, as in Particles: 8-bit steps would show as terraces) and for splat_depth, whose buffers splat_sum adds the splats' colors and weights into (8-bit clamped the sums at 1). render, splat_norm and the output are 8-bit.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on mbpm2 (M2 Pro, 2025.33230), dancer clip with her depth and the stress beat, the demo paused, one look per run: GPU 3.93 ms Points, 4.72 ms Glow, 6.45 ms Surface, all at 60 fps. With the demo running another effect the same build measured 3.04 ms (the GPU clocks up with load), which the docs page says. dev/perf.py on the 0.1.1 build, demo paused (2026-10-24): GPU 3.70 ms Points (95th percentile 4.21), CPU 0.77 ms (1.01), 60 fps held. The change is Python in the audio input's Script CHOP (CPU, once a cook); GPU times on this Mac swing about a third between runs with the GPU's clock (Datamosh 2.8 to 4.0 ms on one build), so the docs page keeps the published numbers. dev/perf.py on the 0.1.2 build, the dancer with her new depth: GPU 2.81 ms Points (95th percentile 4.21), CPU 0.72 ms (0.88), 60 fps held; the docs page keeps the published numbers. 0.1.4 only lays the network out (Network Boxes, positions), drops the unused compute shaders and holds the README to one line per paragraph: dev/probes/structure_diff.py against 0.1.3 finds every other operator, parameter, expression and wire unchanged, so these numbers stand.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say so.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Botsch et al. 2005 and Zwicker et al. 2001 (Surface), Tomasi and Manduchi 1998 (smoothing), Cook 1986 (jitter), the pinhole camera and disparity, the usual handling of flying pixels, pcg2d and pcg3d (Jarzynski and Olano 2020), kinectPointcloud and td-movie-to-particle as unused, and WCAG 2.3.1. A scan of the shaders for known hashes found an integer hash written from memory used constants from Dave Hoskins' Hash without Sine (MIT) that a search couldn't trace, so it was replaced with the paper's pcg2d and pcg3d, here and in the dev prototypes (measured: no cost either way). The beat clock's tempo from the gaps between kicks is credited in both as inter-onset-interval tempo induction, the idea behind Dixon 2001, followed, not copied. 0.1.2: the docs page's preview credit now names Depth Anything 3's Mono-Large model (Apache 2.0), held steady from frame to frame by a scale fit written for this library.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  No Flashing setting: dev/flash.py and dev/flashcount.py under the stress beat (8 beats, 2 bars, 1 drop a second), worst second by the area rule: Points 1.5, Glow 2.0, Surface 2.0 on the dancer (clip 1.0), Points 1.0 on the hands (clip 1.5), 0.5 with no beat (clip 1.0). Glow failed at 5.0 until its dots kept their light as they grew, and Surface measured 3.0 to 3.5 until it got half the swell and burst and a slower bar glide. Re-measured on 0.1.1 with Points: dancer 1.5 (clip 1.0), plaza 0.0 (clip 0.5), and 1.5 with a 140 BPM beat (dev/testbed.py beat=music). The stress beat is Fosfora-shaped, so the raw-audio clock plays no part; Glow and Surface keep their 0.1.0 measurements. Re-measured on 0.1.2 (the new depth clips) with Points, stress beat: dancer 1.5 (clip 1.0), hands 1.0 (clip 1.5), 140 BPM on the dancer 1.5 (clip 1.0). 0.1.3: not re-measured: only the shader clock changed, wrapped every 16384 s (board #4681), which changes its value, not what the effect does with it, apart from one jump each 4.6 h. 0.1.4 only lays the network out (Network Boxes, positions), drops the unused compute shaders and holds the README to one line per paragraph: dev/probes/structure_diff.py against 0.1.3 finds every other operator, parameter, expression and wire unchanged, so these numbers stand.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Color is the picture's, white, or depth as orange near and blue far, which depth also shows as parallax and size as the camera moves. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Song Moments' help and the docs say Fosfora's drop detection is experimental; the README DAT and docs say raw-audio beats come from an experimental kick detector. The wording now says raw-audio beats come from a kick detector and a beat clock that locks to the kicks' tempo, experimental: it can still add or miss beats and which beat starts a bar is a guess.
