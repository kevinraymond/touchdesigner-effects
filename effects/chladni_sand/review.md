# chladni_sand: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Floats for Speed, Settle, Strike, Swell, Depth Focus and Max Distance; Toggles for Idle Strike and Full Screen (states); Menus for Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one); an Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Idle Strike, Depth Focus, Depth Type, Max Distance). Idle Strike's name is Idle; the longest names are Depthfocus and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: figure (a Constant CHOP) holds each mode's weight (the band's rise above its usual level plus 0.15 of it, or Idle Strike's one mode at a time while input 2 has no source) and how hard the plate shakes (Swell times the largest rise, plus Strike times strike_env, at most 1.5); plate reads figure, Speed and Depth Focus (only with input 3 wired); sand reads Settle and the frame's step; idle_count follows Idle Strike; near reads Depth Type and Max Distance; the flash gate's spacing follows Flashing (lib flash_gate()). The only script is parexec (Open Window, Open Docs): actions, not copied values. The model's constants (the seven modes and their peaks, the strike's 2 and 0.35 s, the swell's 1, the shake's limit of 1.5, the bands' 1 s and 4 s, the floor of 0.15, the speed of 0.44 heights a second, Idle Strike's 2 s) live in the builder and the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Speed and Settle (the sand), Strike, Swell and Idle Strike (the music), Depth Focus to Max Distance (input 3), Flashing, then the window. Max Distance grays out unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. Idle Strike stays live with input 2 wired, as Video Vocoder's Idle Sweep does, and its help says it acts only without input 2.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). bands selects the seven bands, bands_now smooths them over 1 s (a Filter CHOP) and bands_usual is their usual level (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s). counts selects beat_count, steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises, and strike_ask takes it or Idle Strike's step (idle_steps, from idle_count) for flash_gate(), whose pulses strike_env (a Trigger CHOP) rings down. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, source is 0: Idle Strike takes over, and the bands drop out of the figure.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The strike's ring is a Trigger CHOP's decay in seconds (0.35), the gate's spacing a Trigger CHOP's re-trigger delay in seconds, the bands' smoothing a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and their usual level a box Filter CHOP 4 s wide, and Idle Strike counts absTime.seconds in 2 s steps. The sand moves by the flow times the frame's step (absTime.stepSeconds, at most 0.1 s), and settles by 1 - exp(-step / Settle), so a dropped frame moves it as far as the frames it stood for.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 207 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired (Idle Strike's count following absTime) and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 25 and 29. The sand's feedback only runs while something pulls show, and plate takes its size from the picture through the resolution menu (quarter), so no expression reads another operator's size.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  show's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). plate is 32-bit float RGBA at a quarter size (the flow and its divergence, signed). sand and its feedback are 32-bit float RGBA at full size: the colors are resampled every frame and the mass runs from 0.05 to 8, and 16-bit floats would round both a little more each frame. near is lib's 16-bit float at a quarter size. figure's eight channels feed plate's uniforms, not a data texture.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build's network (the last build changed only the README DAT's text), three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat: GPU 0.55, 0.55, 0.67 ms (95th percentile 0.84, 0.84, 1.11), CPU 0.28, 0.29, 0.42 ms (0.30, 0.31, 0.57), 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 0.55 ms (0.84) and 0.3 ms. Costliest: show and sand about 0.1-0.2 each (full size), near 0.08, plate 0.07 (a quarter size, seven modes' cosines). With a song as raw audio on input 2: CPU 0.64 ms, of which the audio input 0.38, GPU 0.50. The README DAT says about 0.6 ms.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new chladni_sand.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Chladni 1787 for the figures; Wheatstone 1833 (Phil. Trans.) for the figures as simple modes summed, the formula the plate uses; Stam 1999 for carrying along a flow (semi-Lagrangian), shared with Fluid; Fosfora's seven-band split; Derivative's community tutorial 'Audioreactive particles with Chladni Cymatics' as the same figures before this one, drawn with particles, its project not read; the depth cameras and lib's depth reading; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Each shader's header says the same where it sits. Nothing is called original: the sand as the picture's light with its mass kept, the bands against their usual level as the modes, the strikes through the gate and Idle Strike are said to be written for this library. The docs page has a Patents paragraph (search 2026-10-27: nothing on moving a picture toward nodal lines; private/patents/chladni.md) and names the warp tried first as Not used, on its look.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build's network, one run at a time, each Flashing setting, worst second by the area rule (red where any), Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone). Dance beat (140 BPM): dancer 1.0/1.5/1.0; flowers 1.0 r1.0 / 1.5 r1.5 / 2.5 r1.5; koi 0.5 r1.5 / 1.0 r1.5 / 0.5 r1.5; face 0.5/1.0/1.0. A song as raw audio (m1x_ease_from_drop): dancer 1.0 each; flowers 1.0 r1.0 / 1.0 r1.0 / 1.5 r1.5; koi 1.0 r1.5 / 1.0 r1.0 / 0.5 r1.5. - [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the output is the picture's own colors, moved and lit by how much of its light lies there; the figure shows by brightness (bright lines, dark hollows). No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Without Fosfora's feature bus, the bands its bass, melody and drums stand in for are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page (audio_in()'s, as for Video Vocoder). Raw audio's bands are measured.
