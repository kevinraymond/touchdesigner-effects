# video_vocoder: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Floats for the seven bands (Brilliance to Sub Bass), Drive, Release, Depth Focus and Max Distance; Toggles for Idle Sweep and Full Screen (states); Menus for Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one); an Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Upper Mid, Low Mid, Sub Bass, Idle Sweep, Depth Focus, Depth Type, Max Distance). Idle Sweep's name is Sweep; the longest names are Brilliance and Depthfocus (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: each collapse pass's uB takes its band's slider, the music's gain for its band (Drive times its band in bands_now against bands_usual, or Idle Sweep's wave while input 2 has no source and Idle Sweep is on), and Depth Focus (only with input 3 wired); bands_jump's fall is Release, and the flash gate's spacing follows Flashing (lib flash_gate()); near's Depth Type and Max Distance. The only script is parexec (Open Window, Open Docs): actions, not copied values. The model's constants (each band's swing and limits, the usual level's 4 s, Idle Sweep's 4 s and swing, the jump's 3 a second, the gate's 0.1 s open, the steady level's 1 s) live in the builder, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: the seven bands, Drive to Idle Sweep (the music), Depth Focus to Max Distance (input 3), Flashing, then the window. Max Distance grays out unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. Drive and Idle Sweep stay live without input 2, since Drive also sets how far Idle Sweep swings and Idle Sweep's help says it runs only then; Release stays live too (it only matters with input 2, as its help says).
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(), which gained the seven bands for this effect (sub_bass to brilliance, by name: Fosfora's feature bus, then its bass, melody and drums; analysis channels by their names, then low, mid and high, then the level; raw audio from its own FFTs). bands selects them; rises (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) and ask (their fastest, less 3 a second) ask for a jump, flash_gate() lets one through as often as Flashing allows, and open (a Trigger CHOP) holds it 0.1 s; bands_jump (a Lag CHOP) rises at once only while open and falls over Release, bands_steady (a Filter CHOP) smooths each band over 1 s, bands_now takes the higher, and bands_usual (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s) is each one's usual level. The effect follows levels, not beats, so it reads no counts and no beat pulse; its jumps are the flashes, and they go through the gate. With nothing wired source is 0 and Idle Sweep (or the sliders alone) takes over.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  Release is a Lag CHOP's time in seconds, the gate's spacing and opening a Trigger CHOP's in seconds, the steady level a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and the usual level a box Filter CHOP 4 s wide, and Idle Sweep's wave follows absTime.seconds (one cycle every 4 s). Raw audio's bands are eased over 0.08 s and their peaks fall by half in 4 s, both by the time since the last cook (lib audio_callbacks.py). The picture itself has no state: each frame's octaves come from that frame.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 236 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired (Idle Sweep's wave following absTime) and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in, at Calm and at Off; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 36 and 47. No expression reads another operator's size: the pyramid's levels follow the input through the resolution menu (half), and the shaders read their sizes with textureSize().
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  collapse0's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). The pyramid's levels (g1 to g7) and collapse1 to collapse6 are 32-bit float RGBA: a band is a small difference of two levels, gained up to 3 times and summed over seven passes, and with every gain at 1 the picture must come back as it was (at 32-bit float it came back within 0.13 of an 8-bit step on average; 16-bit float would round each band). near is lib's 16-bit float at a quarter size. No CHOP-to-TOP data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat: GPU 0.79, 0.87, 0.89 ms (95th percentile 1.41, 1.44, 1.45), CPU 0.67, 0.59, 0.55 ms (0.73, 0.71, 0.54), 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 0.9 ms (1.4) and 0.6 ms. Costliest: collapse6 0.15-0.22 (the first pass, waiting on the pyramid), collapse0 0.15-0.17 (the full-size pass), near and in3_top about 0.1 each. With a song as raw audio on input 2: CPU 0.91 ms, of which the audio input 0.40 (its FFTs; the bands' share measured about 0.09 ms in numpy), GPU 0.87.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new video_vocoder.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Dudley 1939 for the channel vocoder; Burt and Adelson 1983 for the Laplacian pyramid (lib pyramid_down.glsl, its binomial kernel, and pyramid.glsl); Sigg and Hadwiger 2005 (GPU Gems 2) for the B-spline expand in four bilinear reads; stoatworks-labs/vocoder (MIT, 2026) as the same idea before this one, for Resolume, whose code was not read; Fosfora's seven-band split; the depth cameras and lib depth reading; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Nothing is called original: judging each band against its usual level, the jumps through the flash gate, Idle Sweep, the coarse octaves' narrower swing and the depth focus are said to be written for this library. Each shader's header says what it does and after whom; nothing was copied or recalled from another implementation. A patent search (2026-10-26) found nothing active that covers gaining an image's pyramid bands by audio: US 9,813,614 (active) needs a blurred high band subtracted from a low one, which this doesn't do, and Fuji's US 5,907,642 and 5,960,123 have expired.

- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the output is the picture's own colors, its detail made stronger or weaker (which raises or lowers small things' saturation with it). No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  The bands Fosfora's bass, melody and drums stand in for without its feature bus are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page. Raw audio's bands are measured, not guessed, and the effect reads no beats, so the beat clock's caveats don't apply.
