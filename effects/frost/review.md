# frost: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Style, Size, Input 3 Holds and Depth Type (discrete choices); Floats for Growth, Crystals, From the Rims, Follow Edges, Freeze Evenly, Thaw by Movement, Thaw by Nearness, Sensitivity, Max Distance, Ice, Glow, Beat Crystals, Loudness and Bass Thaw; an RGB for Tint; a Toggle for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Pulses for Reset, Open Window and Open Docs; an Int for Display. Labels in Title Case (From the Rims and Input 3 Holds keep their small words lower case). The longest names are Depthtype, Beatseeds, Openwindow and Fullscreen (10 or fewer; the save check passes).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: grid and the tensor's smoothing read Size; pane reads Freeze Evenly; seed reads Crystals, From the Rims, Beat Crystals (times the beat and Song Moments' drop when input 2 is wired) and Style; fern reads Follow Edges and its Passes Growth, Loudness and Beat Crystals' spurt; stars its Passes the same; ice and show switch on Style; find reads Input 3 Holds and Sensitivity; near Depth Type and Max Distance; warmth Thaw by Nearness (times Bass Thaw's bass, when input 3 is wired) and Thaw by Movement; the fog passes Size, Growth, Crystals and Beat Crystals; look and fog_look Ice, Glow and Tint. The Parameter Execute DAT reset only pulses ice_fb and fog_fb on Reset or a new Style, copying no values. Values only the inside needs (Kobayashi's and Reiter's constants, the glass's pull, the warmth's times, the look's constants, the fog's sizes) are constants in the builder, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Style, Size, Growth, Crystals, From the Rims, Follow Edges and Reset (the frost); Freeze Evenly, Thaw by Movement, Thaw by Nearness, Input 3 Holds, Sensitivity, Depth Type and Max Distance (cold and warm, input 3); Ice, Glow and Tint (the look); Beat Crystals, Loudness, Bass Thaw and Song Moments (the music); then the window. Follow Edges grays out unless Style is Fern, From the Rims in Fog, Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. The input-3 and music parameters stay live with nothing wired, as no library effect grays a parameter by its wiring; their help says they act on input 3 or input 2.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count and steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame each rises: seed's and fog_level's crystals, fern's and stars' spurt read steps, and glint (a Trigger CHOP from steps) times a beat's glint. level_pick and level_smooth (half a second) ease the loudness for the growth, bass_pick and bass_smooth (a fifth of a second) the bass for Bass Thaw. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, the audio terms fall away (WIRED: audio's source is 0) and the frost grows at Growth.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  Fern's and Stars' steps a frame are RATE times Growth times the frame's step (absTime.stepSeconds, at most 0.1 s), capped at 32, so a second of growth is the same at any frame rate; the seed counts seconds frozen and the chance of a new crystal by the frame's step; the warmth rises and falls and the pane's cold eases by it; Fog's droplets grow, dry and start by it; glint falls over 0.2 s (a Trigger CHOP in seconds). The noise and the crystals' chances are seeded from clock_seconds(), never the raw clock. Movement comes from the input's new frames, held between them (lib warmth()).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 384 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 49 and 57. The Switches (ice, show, edges) cook only the Style's path: in Fog neither the ice nor the structure tensor cooks, in Fern and Stars no fog pass.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  seed, fern, stars and the fog passes are 32-bit float (the phase and temperature that a step's small changes add to, Reiter's vapor near the ice's threshold of 1, the droplets' places in the picture's units); pane, warmth, near, find and the structure tensor's passes are 16-bit float (fractions 0-1, nearness, directions); grid, look and fog_look are 8-bit (the picture); clear is a 1 x 1 32-bit Constant (the start of every Feedback TOP). The lib passes keep their reviewed formats.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs a style on the final build (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, the dancer's depth on input 3, stress beat: Fern GPU 2.50, 2.47, 3.10 ms (95th percentile 4.56, 4.50, 5.25), CPU 0.70, 0.75, 0.76; Stars 2.59, 2.78, 2.80 (4.69, 5.93, 5.86), CPU 0.74, 0.73, 0.72; Fog 2.53, 2.63, 2.64 (3.22, 3.53, 3.41), CPU 0.51, 0.52, 0.51; 60 fps held every time. The page gives the medians. Costliest: fern 1.1 to 1.6 ms, stars 0.9 to 1.1, fog_look 1.2 and fog_level 0.45, pane 0.16 to 0.36. Earlier builds measured 3.2 / 3.6 / 2.8 ms before the look took the ice's slope from screen-space derivatives (Stars' look went from 1.1 to 0.3 ms) and Fog stopped cooking the structure tensor.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new frost.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Kobayashi (Physica D 63, 1993) for Fern's phase field with his constants, Kim and Lin (SCA 2003, linked) for steering the arms' direction, Reiter (Chaos, Solitons and Fractals 23, 2005) for Stars, Beysens and Knobler (PRL 57, 1986) and Fritter, Knobler and Beysens (PRA 43, 1991) for Fog's breath figures, the structure tensor after Kyprianidis, Kang and Doellner, the library's optical flow, warmth and depth reading, Chris Wellons' lowbias32 hash (public domain, linked), WCAG 2.3.1 and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). fern.glsl, stars.glsl and fog_atlas.glsl name their papers where the methods sit. The docs page names the frost before it (frosted-glass jitters, Noisemaker's and ciphrd's DLA, phase-field web components with no picture), the patents searched and how it keeps clear (Clipo US 10,216,945 and Samsung US 11,150,775, touch-cleared frost, Not used; Huawei's pending US 2025/0117558, a phase field moved by a velocity: the ice never moves; private/patents/frost.md), and what was tried and set aside (heat drawn out fast, many crystals, warming the ice's temperature). Original, after a search: the picture as the glass's cold and warmth, the glass drawing the heat away, warmth melting the ice, the droplets kept a grid a size, the frost's look, the beat's crystals, spurts and glints.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  No Flashing setting, so held to Off's rule (nothing added beyond the half-flash margin). dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build, one run at a time, worst second by the area rule, red where any, against the clip alone. Stress beat (8 beats, 2 bars, 1 drop a second): dancer Fern 1.0, Stars 1.0, Fog 1.0 (1.0); flowers 1.0 red 1.0 in all three (1.0 r1.0); plaza 0.5 in all three (0.5); hands 1.5 in all three (1.5); koi Fern 1.0 r1.0, Stars 0.5 r1.5, Fog 0.5 r1.5 (0.5 r1.5). 140 BPM: dancer 1.0 in all three (1.0); koi Fern 1.0 r1.0 (0.5 r1.5). Corners on the dancer (Growth 2, Crystals 1, From the Rims 1, Beat Crystals 1, Loudness 1, Bass Thaw 1, Thaw by Movement 1, Ice 1, Glow 1): 1.0 in all three (1.0; Stars' worst single cell 5.5, the area rule's 1.0). Fern on the koi reaches the half-flash margin: the frost thaws where a fish swims and freezes over again behind it. On the way (board #4846): the beat's glints and spurts measured the same as without them, and every Fog and Stars row the same before and after the look's last changes.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the frost is a cold white (Tint) that reads by brightness, its glints by brightness too. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Raw audio's beat clock and Fosfora's drop detection are labeled experimental in the README DAT (AUDIO, EXPERIMENTAL), on the docs page (Experimental) and in Song Moments' help. The rest is deterministic, the crystals' places hashed.
