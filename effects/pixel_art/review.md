# pixel_art: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Ints for Pixel Size, Colors and Display; Floats for Dither, Saturation, Mosaic Kick, Swell, Subject Colors and Max Distance; Toggles for Hold Palette and Full Screen (states); Pulses for New Palette, Open Window and Open Docs (actions); Menus for Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one). Labels in Title Case (Pixel Size, Hold Palette, New Palette, Mosaic Kick, Subject Colors, Depth Type, Max Distance). Hold Palette's name is Hold, Mosaic Kick's Kick, Subject Colors' Subject; the longest names are Saturation, Newpalette and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the grid's textures take their size from Pixel Size (math.ceil(1280 / Pixel Size), so no expression reads another operator's size), and assign, gather, centers, colors and show read it as a uniform; palette reads Colors, Hold Palette and Saturation; show reads Dither plus the swell (Swell times the loudness's rise over its usual level, only with input 2 wired) and the mosaic's block (Mosaic Kick while kick is up); lab reads Subject Colors only with input 3 wired; near reads Depth Type and Max Distance; the flash gate's spacing follows Flashing (lib flash_gate()). The scripts are parexec (Open Window, Open Docs) and reset (New Palette pulses palette_fb's Reset): actions, not copied values. The method's constants (m 45, the smoothing's 40%, the bilateral sigma, the easing and hold, the annealing's alpha, final temperature and timing, the reveal, the mosaic's block and hold, the swell) live in the builder and the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Pixel Size to Saturation (the art), Hold Palette and New Palette (the palette), Mosaic Kick and Swell (input 2), Subject Colors to Max Distance (input 3), Flashing, then the window. Max Distance grays out unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. Subject Colors stays live without input 3, as Chladni Sand's Depth Focus does, and its help says nothing changes then.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises, flash_gate() lets it through as often as Flashing allows, and kick (a Trigger CHOP) holds the mosaic for 0.15 s. level selects level, level_now smooths it over 1 s (a Filter CHOP) and level_usual is its usual level (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s). The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, source is 0: no beats come, and the swell is 0.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The mosaic's hold is a Trigger CHOP's decay in seconds (0.15), the gate's spacing a Trigger CHOP's re-trigger delay in seconds, the loudness's smoothing a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and its usual level one 4 s wide. The superpixels' colors ease by 1 - exp(-step / 0.2 s) with the frame step (absTime.stepSeconds, capped at 0.1 s), the palette's fade-in adds step / 0.5 s, and the annealing's time at one temperature is counted in seconds (0.06 s, four iterations at 60 fps), so the palette takes shape in the same time at any frame rate. Each frame runs one step of the superpixels and one of the palette, as the paper interleaves them; at a lower frame rate the palette follows a changing picture in fewer steps a second.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 253 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 39 and 43. The Feedback TOPs (centers_fb, colors_fb, index_fb, palette_fb) and the two compute shaders run only while something pulls show, and the grid's sizes follow Pixel Size alone (math.ceil(1280 / Pixel Size)), so no expression reads another operator's size.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  show's output takes the input's format (8-bit from a movie). assign is 8-bit RGBA (which of 9 neighbors, three levels a channel). lab is 16-bit float RGBA at full size (L* 0-100, signed a* and b*, the importance): 16-bit floats keep them to about 0.05. The grid's passes (gather, centers, colors, assoc, index) and the palette's (totals, palette) are 32-bit float RGBA data textures: positions in pixels, Lab colors, sums over up to 409,600 superpixels and the palette's state. near is lib's 16-bit float at a quarter size.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat: GPU 2.25, 2.31, 2.29 ms (95th percentile 2.75, 2.73, 2.72), CPU 0.34 ms each, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 2.3 ms (2.7) and 0.3 ms. Costliest: gather 0.86-0.89 (the compute shader, one workgroup a superpixel; one invocation walking each window cost 2.2 ms before it), assign and show about 0.2 each (full size), assoc, totals and palette about 0.15 each. With a song as raw audio on input 2: CPU 0.67 ms, of which the audio input 0.36, GPU 1.77. Pixel Size 2: GPU 5.62 ms (gather 3.24, totals 1.04); Pixel Size 32: 1.55 ms; Colors 32: 1.99 ms. The README DAT says about 2 ms.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new pixel_art.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Gerstner, DeCarlo, Alexa, Finkelstein, Gingold and Nealen (NPAR 2012) for the method (the SLIC whose color distance is to the palette, the Laplacian smoothing, the bilateral filter on the superpixels, the palette by MCDA, the saturation step, the importance map); Achanta et al. for SLIC (TPAMI 2012, EPFL report 2010); Rose (Proc. IEEE 1998, after Rose, Gurewitz and Fox 1990) for deterministic annealing; Tomasi and Manduchi (ICCV 1998) for the bilateral filter; Hillis and Steele (CACM 1986) for the parallel reductions in shared memory; CIE L*a*b* from sRGB with the D65 white; Bayer (ICC 1973) for the ordered dither's matrix; the Super Nintendo's mosaic; the depth cameras and lib's depth reading; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Each shader's header says the same where it sits (SLIC and the paper in assign, the reductions in gather and totals, MCDA in assoc and palette, the bilateral filter in colors, Bayer and the mosaic in show). The Lab conversions are the standard formulas and matrices. Nothing is called original: running the loop one step a frame on video, the steadying, the fade-in, the red line kept clear, Dither, the mosaic through the gate and the swell are said to be written for this library. The docs page has a Patents paragraph (search 2026-10-27, private/patents/pixel_art.md: nothing on SLIC or the method; EPFL's adaptel patent lapsed) and names as Not used ETH Zurich's patented perceptual downscaling (US 10,325,346) and the lib red guard (tried; it turned the red flowers orange).
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py, one run at a time, each Flashing setting, worst second by the area rule (red where any), Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone). Stress beat: dancer 0.5/1.0/1.5 (1.0); plaza 0.5/0.5/1.0 (0.5); flowers on the final build 1.0 r1.5 / 1.5 r1.5 / 3.0 r3.0 (1.0 r1.0); koi 0.5 r1.0 / 1.0 r1.5 / 2.0 r3.0 (0.5 r1.5); face 0.5 each (0.5); night field 0 (0). Dance beat (140 BPM): flowers 1.0 r1.0 / 1.5 r1.5 / 2.5 r2.5; koi 0.5 r1.0 / 1.0 r1.5 / 2.0 r3.0; face 0.5 each. A song as raw audio (m1x_ease_from_drop): dancer 1.0/1.0/1.5; flowers 1.0 r1.0 / 1.5 r2.0 / 2.5 r3.0; koi 0.5 r1.0 / 1.0 r1.5 / 1.5 r2.5. No music: dancer 1.0 each; flowers 1.0 r1.0 / 0.5 r1.0 / 0.5 r1.0. Corners, spot-checked: flowers at Mosaic Kick 1 1.0 r1.0 / 1.5 r1.5 / 3.0 r3.0; plaza at Mosaic Kick 1 0.5/1.0/2.0 (0.5); with Flashing Off, flowers at Dither 1 and Swell 1, at 24 colors and at Pixel Size 4 1.0 r1.0 each. Mosaic Kick's largest block is 5 art pixels: at 7 the flowers reached 3.5 red flashes a second at Up to 3 per second (FAIL). Up to 3 per second reaches WCAG's 3 on the flowers and koi, the mosaic kicking as often as it allows. What the measuring found and fixed on the way (board #4730, and the red-line finding): the palette's start-up splits flashed (the art now shows the superpixels' colors until the palette is whole, then fades in), and a palette color whose red share drifted across WCAG's 0.8 flipped large areas' red measure at once (each palette color now keeps one side of the line, with hysteresis). dev/reaction_report.py with a song: beat lift 1.00 against the clip's own 0.81 (at Calm the mosaic follows about every other beat), change 0.32% a frame against 0.19% without music.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the output is the picture in a palette picked from its own colors, so it shows what the picture shows, by brightness and shape as much as by hue. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Nothing of its own is unreliable: the superpixels and the palette are worked out from the picture, not guessed at, and the beats and loudness come from audio_in(), whose experimental parts (Fosfora's drops and its stand-in bands, which this effect doesn't read) are labeled on their own pages. No Experimental section is needed.
