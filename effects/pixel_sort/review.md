# pixel_sort: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.1

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Menus for Spans, Sort By, Flips and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Low, High, Edge, Angle, Spin, Beat Kick, Loudness and Length; Ints for Cut Every and Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Cut Every, Sort By, Beat Kick, Song Moments). The longest names are Loudness and Cutevery (8), and the build passes the save-time check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: key's uniforms (Spans and Sort By by menuIndex, Low, High, Edge, Cut Every, the widening), sort's (Angle plus how far Spin has turned plus 180 per flip, as a cosine and sine; Beat Kick while a beat's tears are open), the flip and drop requests' gains (Flips, Song Moments), the drop's whole sort (Length), the widening asked for (Loudness with the level, a build with Song Moments), the spin rate (Spin), and the gate and budget (Flashing). No script copies values. The hysteresis, the widening's limits and glide, Spin's limit, the tears' size and length, the steady period, the flash spacing and the dispatch size stay in the builder and shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Spans to Cut Every (which pixels sort), Sort By to Spin (the sort), Flips to Length (the music), then Flashing, then the window. Low and High gray out unless Spans is Brightness or Depth or Mask, Edge unless it is Edges, and Length with Song Moments off. Cut Every, Sort By, Angle and Spin apply to every kind and stay live.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, bar_count and drop_count, and a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step. Flips follow bars (or beats), whole sorts follow drops; drop_kind marks what passed the gate as a drop's, so drop_pulse starts a whole sort and flip_pulse (the rest) counts toward flip_n, whose parity turns the angle by 180. Beat steps (or steady ticks with nothing wired) drive beat_env, which holds the tears open, and beat_n, a Count CHOP of them, which places them. level is read as a level (level_smooth), build as a level. The beat channel is never read.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  Everything follows seconds: the tears last 0.15 s (a Trigger CHOP decay), the drop's whole sort lasts Length seconds (a Trigger CHOP), the widening glides by a Lag CHOP's slope per second and the level eases over 0.5 s (a Filter CHOP), Spin is integrated per second by a Speed CHOP, and the steady clock is absTime.seconds. The sort itself is the same every frame, with no state in time but the hysteresis, which compares a pixel with its last frame whatever the frame rate. No shader takes the clock.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 59 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 46 and 49. No expression reads another operator's size: key takes its size from the picture, its last frame starts from a 1 x 1 constant read by UV, Cut Every's spacing is worked out in the shader from the output's height, and sort dispatches a fixed 2,560 workgroups (those past the angle's own line count return at once). dev/idle.py on the 0.1.1 build: 0 of 297 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 46 and 49.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  key is 16-bit fixed RGBA: the value sorts at 10 bits, which 8 would round, and the flags fit beside it. sort writes the input's own format (8-bit RGBA from a movie or camera), moving its pixels unchanged. start is a 1 x 1 constant. No data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults: GPU 2.54, 2.73, 2.68 ms (95th percentile 3.04, 3.04, 3.05), CPU 0.27, 0.28, 0.27 ms (0.32, 0.32, 0.31), 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 2.7 ms (3.0) and 0.27 ms (0.32). Costliest: sort about 2.4 ms, key 0.12. One run each of the heaviest settings tried, on an earlier build with the same sort: Whole Lines at Angle 30, 3.42 ms GPU; Angle 0, 2.42 ms. dev/probes/compute_sort.py measured the bare sort of every row of 1280 x 720 at 1.0 ms with 512 threads a workgroup, 1.3 with 1024 and 1.5 with 256. 0.1.1 only lays the network out (Network Boxes, positions), drops the unused pixel shader of its compute-mode sort and holds the README to one line per paragraph: dev/probes/structure_diff.py against 0.1.0 finds every other operator, parameter, expression and wire unchanged, so these numbers stand.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new pixel_sort.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Kim Asendorf's ASDFPixelSort (linked, no license stated, followed), satyarth's pixelsort (linked, MIT, followed for the span kinds and the Low and High defaults), Batcher's bitonic network (AFIPS 1968), the segmented sort by composite key as a widely known technique, Hillis and Steele's scan (CACM 1986), Sobel and the HSV formulas as widely known, lowbias32 (public domain, credited in key.glsl and sort.glsl where it sits), what the survey found and didn't use (MaxMainio's Sort, aaronmylespereira's pixelSorting, Lou Evoy's Pixel Sort TOP, the forum's 2017 Pixel Sorter, elekktronaut's tutorial, linked), the beat clock (Dixon 2001, followed), WCAG 2.3.1, the preview footage and the depth model. ASDFPixelSort's and pixelsort's pages were checked for their licenses and options (2026-10-25). The digital lines at any angle, the compute shader, the hysteresis, the tears and the music are written for this library, and nothing is called original.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second, 600 frames each. Worst second, area rule, Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone): dancer 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5 (1.0); 140 BPM 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0; no beat 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5; plaza 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.0 (0.5); flowers 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.0 (1.0); koi 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5 (0.5); Beat Kick 1 and Loudness 1 on the dancer 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5, flowers 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.0, koi 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0. Flashing Off only: Sort By Hue on the flowers 1.5 and the dancer 1.5 (clip 1.0 each, within the half-flash margin), Edges on the koi 1.0, Whole Lines with Cut Every 120 on the flowers 0.5, Spin 10 (its limit) on the plaza 1.0 (clip 0.5), Depth or Mask (Low 0.3, High 1) on the dancer 1.0. Red: at most 2.0 at Off (Edges on the koi, whose clip makes 1.5 by itself; 1.5 at the defaults), 2.5 at Calm and 2.5 at Up to 3 on the koi (clip 1.5); none on the dancer or the plaza. All within flashcount's bars. Earlier builds failed and were changed (board #4689): Spin 45 and 20 at Flashing Off on the plaza (2.0 and 1.5, clip 0.5), so Spin stops at 10; and loudness widening the spans by a fixed amount sorted the whole picture under a loud build, so it now moves Low and High a share of the way to black and white. 0.1.1 only lays the network out (Network Boxes, positions), drops the unused pixel shader of its compute-mode sort and holds the README to one line per paragraph: dev/probes/structure_diff.py against 0.1.0 finds every other operator, parameter, expression and wire unchanged, so these numbers stand.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the effect only moves the picture's own pixels, draws nothing of its own and no text, so it adds no color the picture didn't have.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page. Hue's shimmer on video is said in Sort By's help and on the page.
