# vhs_crt: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.2.1

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Toggles for Tape, Head Switching, Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Menus for Tape Speed, Signal, Screen (0.2.0: was a toggle, now CRT, LCD, Plasma, E-Ink, None), Mask, Sub-Fields, Ink Mode, Bursts and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Wear, Tracking, Mask Size, Scanlines, Curvature, Glow, Response, Viewing Angle, Ghosting and Length; Ints for Lines and Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Sub-Fields, E-Ink keep their hyphens); the longest names are Headswitch (10) and Scanlines, Curvature, Subfields (9), and the build passes the save-time check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the GLSL uniforms (parent().par.Tape, Wear, Tracking, Headswitch, Lines, Masksize, Scanlines, Curvature, Glow, Response, Angle, Ghosting, and Speed, Signal, Mask, Subfields and Inkmode by menuIndex; Screen == 'crt' for the tube, Screen == 'eink' for the tape's bursts and slip), the raster's height (resolutionh = parent().par.Lines on line_in, tape, deck and signal), the screen shown (shown's index from Screen), the burst requests' gains (Bursts, Moments), the burst's hold (Length), the slip (Moments) and the gate and budget (Flashing). No script copies values. The tape speeds' bandwidths, the sharpening, the raster width, the steady period, the slip, the glow's blur, the flash spacing, the band's height limit, the red guard's knee, the LCD's time constants and angle curves, the sub-field codes and phosphor lags, e-ink's waveform times, ghost shares and refresh shape stay in the builder and shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Tape to Head Switching (the tape), Signal and Lines, Screen to Ghosting (the screens), Bursts to Song Moments (the music), then Flashing, then the window. Tape Speed, Wear, Tracking and Head Switching gray out with Tape off; each screen's settings gray out unless it is chosen: Mask, Scanlines and Curvature are CRT's, Glow CRT's and Plasma's, Mask Size CRT's (unless Mask None), LCD's and Plasma's, Response and Viewing Angle LCD's, Sub-Fields Plasma's, Ink Mode and Ghosting E-Ink's; None leaves none; Length grays out with Bursts Off. dev/probes/vhs_enable.py on the built 0.2.0 .tox prints exactly those for each Screen.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, bar_count and drop_count; a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step. Beat steps drive the kick (beat_env); the burst requests follow bars (or beats) and drops, and the burst's own number (burst_count) seeds its band, which holds still while it lasts. level (noise) and build (tracking) are read as levels. The beat channel is never read.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  Everything follows absTime.seconds: the tape's fields (59.94 a second, for the noise and jitter), the tracking band's crawl and drift, the edge wave and flagging, the steady clock; the kick's envelope (a 0.3 s Trigger CHOP decay), the burst's hold (Length) and fade (0.15 s), the slip (held while the burst is), and the gate and budget (re-trigger delays in seconds). The screens: the LCD's crystal follows by exp(-step / time constant) with the step absTime.stepSeconds (capped at 0.05 s); the plasma's dither turns with its panel frame, int(absTime.seconds x 60) worked out in Python, and its motion is the optical flow divided by the seconds between the input's frames (lib flow_speed()); e-ink's update number and phase are absTime.seconds over the mode's waveform time (0.45, 0.29, 0.12 s), worked out in Python too (in double precision: a 32-bit float of the seconds since TouchDesigner started lost updates after 12 hours, board #4680), and its full refresh is a Trigger CHOP envelope in seconds. Nothing counts frames. 0.2.1: The tape's clock (fields' noise, the tracking band's crawl, the time base's wobble) is lib clock_seconds(), wrapped every 16384 s so a 32-bit uniform keeps it to 1 ms (board #4681): before, the noise would have dropped to 30 fields a second after about two days of uptime. At each wrap (every 4.6 h) the band jumps once to another height, as a tracking slip would; it is one change, not a flash.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final 0.1.0 build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 61 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 40 and 41. The raster's height is an expression on the Lines parameter, not on another operator's size, and the glow's size follows the source through the resolution menu (eighth). 0.2.0: dev/idle.py on the final 0.2.0 build at each Screen (CRT, LCD, Plasma, E-Ink): 0 of 125 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired, every time; the control cooked 42-46 (69 for Plasma, whose optical flow cooks only then). The screens hang off one Switch TOP (shown), so only the chosen one cooks; their Feedback TOPs start from a 1 x 1 constant and no size expression reads another operator.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  The raster (line_in, tape, deck, signal) is 16-bit float: YIQ's color channels go negative, and the filters sum dozens of taps. The glow's source is 16-bit float, being linear light (8-bit bands in the dark). The LCD's crystal (lcd_state) and e-ink's state (eink_state) are 16-bit float: they creep toward their targets by small steps each frame and hold ghosts of a few percent, which 8 bits would stall or band. The optical flow, pace and motion are 32-bit float (lib: speeds and times), and still, where every Feedback TOP starts, is 1 x 1 32-bit. Every screen's output (screen, lcd, plasma, eink) follows the picture's format, its input 0, 8-bit for an 8-bit picture. No data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final 0.1.0 build, three runs (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults: GPU 3.45, 2.94, 2.91 ms (95th percentile 4.35, 3.45, 3.38), CPU 0.42, 0.30, 0.30 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the median, 2.9 ms GPU and 0.30 ms CPU. Costliest: tape 1.0 ms, deck 0.9 ms. An earlier build measured 2.14 ms; GPU times on this Mac swing about a third between runs. 0.2.0: dev/perf.py on the final 0.2.0 build, same machine and size. Defaults (CRT), three runs: GPU 1.89, 1.88, 2.12 ms (95th percentile 2.53, 2.57, 2.65), CPU 0.41, 0.37, 0.32 ms, 60 fps each; the page gives the medians, 1.9 ms (2.6) and 0.37 ms (0.45), and notes 0.1.0's 2.9 ms (this Mac swings about a third between runs). One run each for the other screens: LCD GPU 2.09 ms (CPU 0.30), E-Ink 1.84 ms (0.41), Plasma 3.80 ms (0.55; plasma 1.19, lk4 0.64, lk8 0.33, motion 0.15), all at 60 fps.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new vhs_crt.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name NTSC (FCC 1953, SMPTE 170M) and VHS's published bandwidths; ntsc-rs (MIT OR ISC OR Apache-2.0, checked in its Cargo.toml), ntscqt and ntsc (Apache-2.0) and composite-video-simulator (GPL-2.0), whose source was read for the order of a tape's passes and its causal filters, no code taken (ntsc-rs's ntsc.rs and filter.rs were read; the shaders here are written from the bandwidths, and the cutoffs differ); the textbook NTSC receiver; CRT-Geom (GPL) and Lottes' shader (public domain) for brightness-dependent Gaussian scanlines and masks, followed, not copied; halation as the common blurred mix; lowbias32 (public domain, credited in lib/glsl/common.glsl where it sits); the components seen in the survey; the beat clock (Dixon 2001, followed); WCAG 2.3.1. The bursts, the slip and the red guard are written for this library. A scan of the shaders found only lowbias32, the standard YIQ matrices, one-pole filter poles, Gaussian and Hann-windowed sinc kernels. 0.2.0 adds, in both: the LCD's twisted-nematic response (Jakeman and Raynes, Physics Letters A 39(1), 1972, citation checked) and the gray-scale inversion and washout of TN panels as widely described (the curves are this library's, shaped by eye, and say so); the plasma's sub-field gray scale and dynamic false contours (Yamaguchi, Masuda, Kohgami et al., J. SID 4(4) 263-270, 1996; citation checked, Mikoshiba's authorship not confirmed, so 'et al.'), the redundant code as one later panels used, the phosphor decay figures from US patent 7,138,965 (green ~10 ms to a tenth, blue 1 ms or less; red's assumed, said so); e-ink's waveform modes and times from the IT8951 controller's documentation (GL16 450 ms, DU4 290 ms, A2 120 ms; checked in the it8951 package's docs and ESPHome's it8951_defs.h); the ghost model as this library's; the library's Lucas-Kanade flow (Lucas and Kanade 1981) behind plasma's motion. The new shaders (panel, lcd_state, lcd, plasma, eink_state, eink) are written here; the Bayer 4 x 4 matrix is the standard ordered-dither matrix.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final 0.1.0 build, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second, 600 frames each. Worst second, area rule, Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone): dancer 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5 (1.0); dancer at 140 BPM 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.0 (1.0); dancer with no beat 1.0 at every setting (1.0); plaza 0.5 / 0.5 / 1.0 (0.5); flowers 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5 (1.0); koi 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5 (0.5); flowers at Wear 1, Tracking 1, EP 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.5 (1.0). Red: 0 everywhere (the flowers alone 1.0, the koi 1.5). All within flashcount's bars. Earlier builds failed and were changed (board #4678): a roll on drops (3.5 at Up to 3, 3.0 at Calm), composite's field-to-field phase flip and the tape's noise on reds near WCAG's 80% line (red 2.0 at Off on the flowers), the mask's red stripes over orange (red 2.5 at Off, worst case). 0.2.0: the new screens on the final build (e-ink's update numbering fixed, below), same stress beat, Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second, area rule (clip alone as in 0.1.0): dancer LCD 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.0, Plasma 1.0 / 1.0 / 2.0, E-Ink 1.0 / 1.0 / 2.0; plaza LCD 0.5 / 0.5 / 1.0, Plasma 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.0, E-Ink 0.0 / 1.0 / 2.0; flowers LCD 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5, Plasma 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5, E-Ink 1.0 / 1.0 / 2.0; koi LCD 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5, Plasma 0.5 / 1.5 / 1.5, E-Ink 1.0 / 1.0 / 2.0. Red 0 everywhere. All within flashcount's bars. The CRT path is unchanged from 0.1.0 (the tape, signal and screen shaders and the burst chain are the same; the tape only skips its burst and slip on e-ink), so its table stands. An earlier 0.2.0 build failed the spirit of Off on e-ink: koi 2.0 at Off (clip 0.5 by brightness), repeatable; Ghosting 0 or 16 Grays gave 0.5. Cause: the state and face passes each worked out the update from absTime.seconds as a 32-bit uniform (1/256 s at 12 h of uptime) and disagreed near an update's start, so the state skipped it and the face replayed the last one. Fixed by counting updates in Python and storing the one each pixel took (board #4680); then koi 1.0 at Off, the same as the CRT and the LCD there. Plaza's plasma reaches 5.5-6.5 in its single worst cell (bottom edge: false contours over the head-switching lines), far under WCAG's area. 0.2.1: not re-measured: only the shader clock changed, wrapped every 16384 s (board #4681), which changes its value, not what the effect does with it, apart from one jump each 4.6 h.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the bleeding, the noise and the mask are the look, and the bursts carry their timing by a band of torn lines across the picture. No text is drawn. The red guard keeps very saturated reds short of WCAG's saturated-red line.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page (a kick detector and a beat clock that can still add or miss beats; which beat starts a bar is a guess).
