# particles: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Show, Find, Depth Type, Emit From and Color (discrete choices); Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen; Floats for Sensitivity, Stand Out, Far Limit, Max Distance, Rate, Life, Size, Speed, Drift, Turbulence, Glow, Backdrop, Loudness, Beat Bursts and Bar Gusts, Int for Display; Pulses for Capture Background, Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case; the longest names are Turbulence and Depthtype (10 and 9), and the build passes the save-time check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the uniforms of near (Depth Type, Max Distance), find (Find, Stand Out, Far Limit), motion and subject (Sensitivity, Find), sim (Rate, Loudness, Beat Bursts, Life, Speed, Emit From, Drift, Turbulence, Song Moments, Size, Color), gust (Bar Gusts), look (Backdrop, Glow, Show), and the Switch TOPs plate_in and drift_in (Find). The one script, capture_exec, counts Capture Background presses in a Constant CHOP (an event, not a copy of a value). The particle count, the subject's resolution, the burst's and shockwave's size and timing, Sensitivity's range and the labels stay in the builder; the dice, swirl scale, fade times and Movement's timings in the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Show; finding the subject (Find, Capture Background, Sensitivity, Stand Out, Far Limit, Depth Type, Max Distance); the particles (Emit From to Turbulence); the look (Color, Glow, Backdrop); the music (Loudness, Beat Bursts, Bar Gusts, Song Moments); then the window. Capture Background is grayed out unless Find is Background; Sensitivity unless Auto, Movement or Background; Stand Out unless Auto or Stand Out; Far Limit and Depth Type unless a depth mode; Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count; a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step, and Trigger CHOPs turn the steps into a beat's burst and kick (beat_env, 0.25 s) and a drop's shockwave (drop_env, 1.2 s), envelopes sim reads every frame, so a step it misses still shows. The wind's angle follows bar_count (a count) through a Lag CHOP; level and build go through Lag CHOPs too. The beat channel is never read.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  sim moves, ages and fades every particle by absTime.stepSeconds (capped at 0.1 s after a stall), births come to Rate per second times that step, the turbulence follows absTime.seconds, Movement's background and trail are time constants in seconds over the same step, and the burst, shockwave and wind are envelopes and lags in seconds. The frame number only seeds the dice.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final 0.1.0 build (node viewer off, nothing downstream, nobody viewing /tdlib_dev): 0 of 99 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with nothing wired and with the clip, its depth on input 3 and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 54 and 57. Background's passes (plate, gain, gain_sum, drift) sit behind Switch TOPs and cook only while Find is Background.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  The particles' three buffers, their Feedback TOPs, motion, plate, gain, drift, emit and the Analyze TOPs are 32-bit float (data: positions, ages, backgrounds, counts, averages). near, find, small, subject and depth_stats are 16-bit float (nearness and color, where 8 bits would band the comparisons; subject's label code). render and glow are 16-bit float, since overlapping particles add up past 1 and look.glsl rolls them off softly. dot and the output are 8-bit.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final 0.1.0 build (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, stress beat, defaults: the dancer with its depth on input 3, GPU 2.47 ms (95th percentile 3.64), CPU 0.82 ms (0.90), 60 fps held (render 0.87, sim 0.49, glow 0.44, find 0.28, near 0.14); at Rate 30000 and Life 4, GPU 2.72 ms; the plaza with no depth (Auto, Movement), GPU 2.74 ms; the plaza on Background, GPU 2.64 ms (gain 0.20). The first histogram (one pixel per bin) took 5 ms and was split into rows before release.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new particles.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  No Flashing setting: particles fade in over 0.12 s and out over the second half of their lives, and the music moves particles and adds them (loudness, bursts and kicks, gusts) rather than lighting the picture. Measured anyway with dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final 0.1.0 build, one run at a time, the depth showing the clip's frame, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second, 600 frames each, depth from Depth Anything 3 Base. Worst second, area rule: dancer with its depth 1.0 (clip alone 1.0); hands with their depth 0.5 (clip 1.5); dancer, no beat, 0.5 (clip 1.0); plaza with no depth (Movement) 0.0 (clip 0.5). No red flashes. Never above the footage, under WCAG's 3.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  The particles' colors are the picture's or one of orange, blue and white, as decoration. Show on Subject marks the subject in bright orange and the depth beside it in dim blue, so brightness separates them as well as hue, and its label says in words how the subject was found: the library's 5 x 7 font at 2 pixels per font pixel at 720 high (scaling with the picture), white on a dark plate.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beat detection in the README DAT and the docs page. Each way of finding the subject states its limits in the README DAT and the docs page (Stand Out: people cut off by the left or right edge, feet; Movement: still people fade, flat colors and moving shadows, a moving camera; Background: the camera and light must stay put), the first in Find's help too.
