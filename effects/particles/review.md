# particles: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.3.1

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Show, Find, Depth Type, Behavior, Emit From and Color (discrete choices); Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen; Floats for Sensitivity, Stand Out, Far Limit, Max Distance, Rate, Life, Size, Speed, Drift, Direction, Turbulence, Glow, Backdrop, Loudness, Beat Bursts and Bar Gusts, Int for Display; Pulses for Capture Background, Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case; the longest names are Turbulence (10) and Direction and Depthtype (9), and the build passes the save-time check. 0.3.0 adds the standard Flashing menu (new_effect(flashing=True)). 0.3.1 changes two defaults only (Stand Out 0.10, Far Limit 0.15); no parameter added or renamed.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the uniforms of near (Depth Type, Max Distance), find (Find, Stand Out, Far Limit), motion and subject (Sensitivity, Find), air (Behavior, Drift, Direction, Turbulence, Song Moments), sim (Behavior, Rate, Loudness, Beat Bursts, Life, Speed, Emit From, Drift, Direction, Turbulence, Song Moments, Size, Color), gust (Bar Gusts), look (Backdrop, Glow, Show), and the Switch TOPs plate_in and drift_in (Find) and air_in (Behavior). The one script, capture_exec, counts Capture Background presses in a Constant CHOP (an event, not a copy of a value). The particle count, the subject's resolution, the burst's, pulse's and shockwave's size and timing, Push and Flow's births (FIELD x Rate), the air's swirl and blur sizes, Sensitivity's range and the labels stay in the builder; the dice, swirl scale, fade and settle times, Push's resting glow and swell and Movement's timings in the shaders, off the page. 0.3.0: the sparks follow spark_env (the gate's output) through sim's uLook.w; their size and fade are #defines in sim.glsl (SPARK, SPARK_FADE), the burst rate a builder constant. 0.3.1: find's Far Limit uniform is an expression of the page, Far Limit while input 3 is wired, else the larger of Far Limit and BRIGHT_FAR (0.5, a builder constant): brightness standing in for depth keeps its own floor.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Show; finding the subject (Find, Capture Background, Sensitivity, Stand Out, Far Limit, Depth Type, Max Distance); the particles (Behavior to Turbulence); the look (Color, Glow, Backdrop); the music (Loudness, Beat Bursts, Bar Gusts, Song Moments); then the window. Capture Background is grayed out unless Find is Background; Sensitivity unless Auto, Movement or Background; Stand Out unless Auto or Stand Out; Far Limit and Depth Type unless a depth mode; Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters; Emit From unless Behavior is Emit; Drift, Direction and Bar Gusts while Behavior is Push (still air). Speed stays live in every behavior, since a beat's burst leaves the subject at it in Push and Flow too. 0.3.0: Flashing comes after Song Moments, before the window, as in every flashing effect. 0.3.1: no change to sections or grayed-out rules.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count; a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step, and Trigger CHOPs turn the steps into a beat's burst and kick (beat_env, 0.25 s) and a drop's shockwave (drop_env, 1.2 s), envelopes sim reads every frame, so a step it misses still shows. Push and Flow's pulse follows beat_rate, a Slope CHOP of the beat_env envelope (not of a pulse), times the frame's step. The wind's angle follows bar_count (a count) through a Lag CHOP; level and build go through Lag CHOPs too. The beat channel is never read. The audio input (lib/audio_callbacks.py) now gives raw audio's beats from a beat clock locked to the kicks' tempo and Fosfora's level from its bass (#4652), checked by dev/selftest.py and dev/beat_bench.py --selftest. Sparks ask through beat_count's steps (spark_ask) and the flash gate; nothing reads the beat channel.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  sim moves, ages and fades every particle by absTime.stepSeconds (capped at 0.1 s after a stall), births come to Rate per second times that step, the shove's speed is its move divided by the step and the particles' own speed dies away over time constants in seconds; the air's swirl and Emit's turbulence follow absTime.seconds; the pulse moves the field by the envelope's change over the step; Movement's background and trail are time constants in seconds over the same step; the burst, shockwave and wind are envelopes and lags in seconds. The frame number only seeds the dice. 0.3.0: a spark's size decays by exp(-step / SPARK_FADE) with the step in seconds; spark_env holds for BURST_TIME seconds.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the 0.2.0 code (node viewer off, nothing downstream, nobody viewing /tdlib_dev), once at each Behavior (dev/idle.py gained Par=value for it): 0 of 109 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with nothing wired and with the clip, its depth on input 3 and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 58 to 65. Background's passes sit behind Switch TOPs and cook only while Find is Background, the air's (subject_wide, subject_middle, air) only while Behavior is Push or Flow. dev/idle.py on the final 0.3.0 build (with the spark gate): 0 of 113 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 62 and 65. dev/idle.py on the 0.3.1 build: 0 of 113 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 62 and 65.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  The particles' four buffers, their Feedback TOPs, motion, plate, gain, drift, emit, subject_middle (emit's position sums, blurred), air (a stream function whose neighboring pixels are differenced) and the Analyze TOPs are 32-bit float (data: positions, ages, backgrounds, counts, averages). near, find, small, subject, subject_blur, subject_wide and depth_stats are 16-bit float (nearness and color, where 8 bits would band the comparisons; subject's label code). render and glow are 16-bit float, since overlapping particles add up past 1 and look.glsl rolls them off softly. dot and the output are 8-bit.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the 0.2.0 code (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, stress beat, defaults but for the setting named, 60 fps held in every run: the dancer with its depth on input 3, Emit GPU 2.36 ms (95th percentile 3.11), CPU 1.28 ms (1.42) (render 1.05, sim 0.59, glow 0.42, find 0.29); Push 2.98 ms, Flow 3.05 ms (subject_wide 0.35-0.38 of the difference; a Pre-Shrink on it changed nothing, so it was left off); the plaza with no depth (Auto, Movement), Emit 2.35, Push 3.08, Flow 3.23 ms; the plaza on Background 3.09 ms (gain 0.26); Rate 30000 and Life 4 on the dancer 2.60 ms. dev/perf.py on the 0.3.0 code, demo paused, median of three runs: GPU 2.62 ms (95th percentile 3.05), CPU 1.05 ms (1.38), 60 fps held; render 1.1, sim 0.6, glow 0.5 ms. dev/perf.py on the 0.3.1 build, the dancer with her new depth: GPU 2.23 ms (95th percentile 2.60), CPU 0.89 ms (1.02), 60 fps held, under the published numbers, which stand: the change is two defaults and one uniform expression.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new particles.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name curl noise (Bridson et al. 2007) on TouchDesigner's simplex noise, and the same paper's boundaries for Push and Flow's air going round the subject (also credited in air.glsl); the staggered grid (Harlow and Welch 1965) for taking the air's velocity on a cell's sides (sim.glsl's air()), the midpoint rule as a standard method, position-based dynamics (Müller et al. 2006) for Push's shove; the PCG hash (Jarzynski and Olano 2020), running-average background subtraction, the depth TOPs' formats, the 5 x 7 font, the Palette's particlesGpu as unused and WCAG 2.3.1. Stand Out's row search, the exposure drift, Flow's particles waiting behind the subject and the beat's pulse are written for this library (no search for prior art was made, so none is called original). A scan of the shaders for known hashes and formulas found only the PCG hash. 0.3.0: the beat clock's tempo from the gaps between kicks is credited in both as inter-onset-interval tempo induction, the idea behind Dixon 2001, followed, not copied. The sparks are written for this library. 0.3.1: the docs page's preview credit now names Depth Anything 3's Mono-Large model (Apache 2.0), held steady from frame to frame by a scale fit written for this library.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.

- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  The particles' colors are the picture's or one of orange, blue and white, as decoration; Push shows a shove by brightness and size, not color. Show on Subject marks the subject in bright orange and the depth beside it in dim blue, so brightness separates them as well as hue, and its label says in words how the subject was found: the library's 5 x 7 font at 2 pixels per font pixel at 720 high (scaling with the picture), white on a dark plate.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beat detection in the README DAT and the docs page. Each way of finding the subject states its limits in the README DAT and the docs page (Stand Out: people cut off by the left or right edge, feet; Movement: still people fade, flat colors and moving shadows, a moving camera; Background: the camera and light must stay put), the first in Find's help too. The Behaviors section and the README DAT say that arms and other thin parts let a little of Push and Flow's air through. 0.3.0: raw-audio beats are now described as a kick detector and a beat clock, experimental (it can still add or miss beats; which beat starts a bar is a guess), in the README DAT's audio section, the README DAT and the docs page.
