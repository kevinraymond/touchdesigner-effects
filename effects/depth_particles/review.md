# depth_particles: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Show, Find, Polarity, Emit From and Color (discrete choices); Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen; Floats for Stand Out, Far Limit, Rate, Life, Size, Speed, Drift, Turbulence, Glow, Backdrop, Loudness, Beat Bursts and Bar Gusts, Int for Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case; the longest name is Turbulence (10), and the build passes the save-time check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the uniforms of near (Polarity), find (Find, Stand Out, Far Limit), sim (Rate, Loudness, Beat Bursts, Life, Speed, Emit From, Drift, Turbulence, Song Moments, Size, Color), gust (Bar Gusts) and look (Backdrop, Glow, Show). No script copies values. The particle count, the people's resolution, the burst's and the shockwave's size and timing stay in the builder, and the dice, swirl scale and fade times in sim.glsl, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Show; finding the people (Find, Stand Out, Far Limit, Polarity); the particles (Emit From to Turbulence); the look (Color, Glow, Backdrop); the music (Loudness, Beat Bursts, Bar Gusts, Song Moments); then the window. Stand Out is grayed out unless Find is Stand Out.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count; a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step, and Trigger CHOPs turn the steps into a beat's burst and kick (beat_env, 0.25 s) and a drop's shockwave (drop_env, 1.2 s), envelopes sim reads every frame, so a step it misses still shows. The wind's angle follows bar_count (a count) through a Lag CHOP; level and build go through Lag CHOPs too. The beat channel is never read.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  sim moves, ages and fades every particle by absTime.stepSeconds (capped at 0.1 s after a stall), births come to Rate per second times that step, the turbulence follows absTime.seconds, and the burst and shockwave are Trigger CHOP envelopes in seconds. The frame number only seeds the dice.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final 0.1.0 build (node viewer off, nothing downstream, nobody viewing /tdlib_dev): 0 of 61 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with nothing wired and with the clip, its depth on input 3 and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 42 and 45. Two earlier runs saw sample and source cook, likely a network pane open inside /tdlib_dev at the time; it didn't recur in four runs.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  The particles' three buffers, their Feedback TOPs, emit and the two Analyze TOPs are 32-bit float (data: positions, ages, averages). near and find are 16-bit float (nearness, where 8 bits would band the Stand Out test). render and glow are 16-bit float, since overlapping particles add up past 1 and look.glsl rolls them off softly. dot and the output are 8-bit.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final 0.1.0 build (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, the dancer clip with its depth (Depth Anything 3 Base) on input 3 and the stress beat, defaults: GPU 2.35 ms (95th percentile 2.83), CPU 0.84 ms (1.05), 60 fps held; render 0.94, glow 0.52, look 0.49, sim 0.38, find 0.14. At Rate 30000 and Life 4: GPU 2.44 ms, so particle count barely matters.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new depth_particles.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  No Flashing setting: particles fade in over 0.12 s and out over the second half of their lives, and the music moves particles and adds them (loudness, bursts and kicks, gusts) rather than lighting the picture. Measured anyway with dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final 0.1.0 build, one run at a time, the depth showing the clip's frame, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second (a burst and kick 8 times a second, a shockwave every second, loud throughout), 600 frames each, depth from Depth Anything 3 Base. Worst second, area rule: dancer with its depth 1.0 (clip alone 1.0); hands with their depth 0.5 (clip 1.5); dancer, no beat, 0.5 (clip 1.0). No red flashes. Never above the footage, under WCAG's 3.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  The particles' colors are the picture's or one of orange, blue and white, as decoration. Show on People marks the people in bright orange and the depth beside them in dim blue, so brightness separates them as well as hue. No text is drawn.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beat detection in the README DAT and the docs page. Find's two limits (people cut off by the left or right edge drop out, feet merge with the floor) are stated in the README DAT and the docs page, the first in Find's help too.
