# reaction_diffusion: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Pattern, Size, Grow On, Depth Type, Colors and Flashing (discrete choices); Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Floats for Follow Edges, Speed, Sensitivity, Max Distance, Ink Level, Contrast, Beat Kick and Loudness; an Int for Display; Pulses for Reset, Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Follow Edges, Grow On, Depth Type, Max Distance, Ink Level, Beat Kick, Song Moments). The longest names are Depthtype and Inklevel (9 and 8), and the build passes the save-time name check.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: grid's resolution and the tensor's smoothing (Size); react's stretch (Follow Edges) and its Passes (Speed, with the frame step); seed's feed and kill rates (Pattern); where's Grow On, Sensitivity, Contrast, Ink Level, Light on Dark (Colors) and the loudness (Loudness); grow_from's index (Grow On); near's Depth Type and Max Distance, find's Sensitivity; look's Pattern, Colors, Beat Kick and Song Moments; the flash gate's Flashing. The one script, reset, pulses two Feedback TOPs' Reset on the Reset pulse: an action, not a copied value. The model's constants (diffusion rates, the step, the stretch, the seeds, the easing times, the ripple's timing and width) live in the builder, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Pattern to Reset (the pattern), Grow On to Max Distance (where it grows), Ink Level to Colors (the ink), Beat Kick to Song Moments (the music), then Flashing and the window. Sensitivity grays out for Everywhere, Depth Type unless Grow On is Depth or Mask, Max Distance unless also Depth Type is Distance in Meters.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and drop_count, a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step, ask renames them beat and drop, and flash_gate() (flash_limit, flash) lets them through as often as Flashing allows: beat_now starts ring (a Trigger CHOP ramping 0 to 1 over 0.6 s) and counts beat_n (where each ripple starts), drop_now starts drop_ring. level is read as a level (level_smooth). Nothing reads the one-frame beat channel. With nothing wired no step comes, and the loudness term is 0 (it needs a source).
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The model runs Speed x 1200 of its time units a second: react's Passes follow the frame step (absTime.stepSeconds, capped at 0.05 s and 48 steps), so a slow frame runs more steps. moved, exposure, where and tensor_ease ease by 1 - exp(-step / time) with the frame step (capped at 0.1 s), so their times are seconds at any frame rate. The fade-in counts seconds in exposure. A ripple crosses in 0.6 s and a drop's in 1.2 s (Trigger CHOPs), the loudness eases over 0.5 s (a Filter CHOP). seed's draws follow the clock (clock_seconds(), 30 draws a second).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 317 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 43 and 46. The pattern's Feedback TOPs pass on the last frame only when something pulls them, so an unused effect holds its pattern still and picks it up again when shown. grow_from (a Switch TOP) cooks only what Grow On needs: moved and grid_fb for Movement, find for Depth, near for Mask, none of them for Everywhere and Brightness. No expression reads another operator's size: grid's size follows Size through its Output Resolution menu, and the stateful passes read their feedback by UV, so a new Size scales the pattern instead of resetting it.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  grid and look are pictures (the input's format; grid 8-bit from a movie). The pattern's state (still, seed, react, state_fb) is 32-bit float RGBA: a Gray-Scott step changes U and V by about 1e-4, which 16-bit float (a step of 2.4e-4 at 0.5) would round away. exposure is 32-bit float, one pixel (the range and the seconds since the start). tensor, tensor_h, tensor_v, tensor_ease and flow are lib's 16-bit float; where is 16-bit float RGBA (two 0-1 values eased by small steps, and its alpha says it has been set), moved mono 16-bit float. near and find are lib's 16-bit float. No CHOP-to-TOP data textures.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final build, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults (Lines, Medium), stress beat: GPU 4.04, 4.34, 3.78 ms (95th percentile 5.81, 5.74, 4.51), CPU 0.46, 0.46, 0.62 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 4.0 ms (5.7) and 0.46 ms. Costliest: react 3.1 to 3.7 ms (31 steps of the model a frame at 60 fps), then look and grid about 0.2 each. One run each: Size Fine 7.51 ms (react 6.56), Coarse 2.03 ms (react 0.97, look 0.66). During development one step measured 0.055 ms at 640 x 360 and 0.19 ms at 1280 x 720 (board #4701).
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new reaction_diffusion.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Turing 1952 (DOI linked), Gray and Scott 1983 (DOI linked) for the model and Pearson 1993 (DOI linked) for its map of patterns, saying the feed and kill rates here were found by running the model over Pearson's region and the diffusion rates are the usual ones; Witkin and Kass 1991 (DOI linked, checked against the ACM record) for steered diffusion; Kyprianidis, Kang and Doellner 2009 and Kyprianidis and Doellner 2011 with Jaehne, Scharr and Koerkel 1999 for the structure tensor (lib, shared with Paint); Catmull-Rom 1974 and lowbias32 (public domain, linked) from lib common.glsl; the depth cameras and lib depth reading; the TouchDesigner versions seen in the survey and not used (TD-Toxes' reactionDiffusion, MIT and tutorial-derived; paid Gumroad versions; a Reddit .tox with no license); Dixon 2001 for the beat clock, followed; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). Each shader's header names its source. The Gray-Scott equations are the model's own; the steering's directional second differences, the seeds, the stretch and easing of the tone, the line widths, the fade-in and the ripple are written for this library and not called original (the directional discretization is described as a usual way, with no one source followed). Nothing was copied or recalled from another implementation.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final build, the demo paused, stress beat of 8 beats, 2 bars and 1 drop a second, worst second by the area rule, Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second (clip alone): dancer 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.5 (1.0); 140 BPM 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5; 140 BPM Beat Kick 1 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0; no beat, Off 1.0; plaza 0.5 / 1.5 / 2.5 (0.5); flowers 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 (1.0, red 1.0); koi 1.0 / 1.0 / 2.0 (0.5, red 1.5). At Calm only: flowers Over the Picture 1.0, red 1.0; dancer Beat Kick 1 and Loudness 1 2.0; Dots 1.5; Coral 2.0; Fine 1.0; Coarse 2.0; Grow On Movement 1.5; Grow On Depth 1.5. Each within its setting's rule (Off within flashcount's half-flash margin on the koi). Found and fixed on the way (board #4702): the pattern growing in on blank paper, 1.5 to 2.5 in its worst second on a still frame, so it starts as an even tone and crossfades into the grown pattern (0.5); the stripes turning and thickening after drifting smoke, 1.5 over the clip's own with no beat, so the tensor eases over 0.5 s (lib structure_tensor(ease=)) and the ink over 0.25 s (then the clip's own); a ripple on every beat, 2.0 at 140 BPM with Beat Kick 1 and 2.5 under the stress beat, so beats and drops go through the flash gate as one channel (gated apart, a beat's and a drop's ripple each came once a second and Beat Kick 1 with Loudness 1 measured 3.0 at Calm; as one, 2.0). Red: the drawn colors make none; Over the Picture keeps the picture's own.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color. Ink on Paper is near-black on warm white, Light on Dark white on black, Over the Picture the picture's own colors under near-black ink, and Orange and Blue is the library's colorblind-safe pair, blue ink on orange, which also differ in brightness. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page. The pattern lagging behind a moving picture is said plainly in the README DAT and the docs page (it is how the effect works, not unreliable).
