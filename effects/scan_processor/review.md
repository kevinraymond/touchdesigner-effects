# scan_processor: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Scan, Colors and Flashing (discrete choices; Flashing the standard one); Floats for Amount, Speed, Beat Kick, Idle and Beam; Ints for Lines and Display; a Toggle for Full Screen (a state); Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Beat Kick, Open Window). Beat Kick's name is Kick; the longest names are Fullscreen and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: levels (a Constant CHOP) holds each generator's level (the band smoothed over 1 s times 0.6 plus its rise above its usual level, at most 1; or Idle times a slow sine per generator while input 2 has no source) and the signed strike (strike_env times the sign from strikes' count, times Idle while nothing is wired); drive reads levels for the low, middle and high bands, Scanimate's bend (Amount) and FM's beta (Amount, Beat Kick, at most 0.85); phase reads levels and Speed; yokes reads levels, drive, Scan, Amount and Beat Kick; yokes' width follows Lines; scan reads Lines, Beam, Scan, Amount (the vertical yoke's reach) and drive; show reads Colors and drive; idle_count follows Idle; the flash gate's spacing follows Flashing (lib flash_gate()). The only script is parexec (Open Window, Open Docs): actions, not copied values. The model's constants (the generators' cycles and drifts, the yokes' strengths, the fold guard's 0.6, the twist's and bend's scales, FM's 7 pleats and its beta's limit, the glow caps, the bands' 1 s and 4 s, the strike's 0.35 s, Idle's 2 s) live in the builder and the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Scan, Amount, Speed, Beat Kick and Idle (the scan and the music); Lines and Beam (the raster); Colors; Flashing; then the window. Every parameter applies to all three scans (Speed moves the generators each one reads, Beat Kick strikes each its own way), so nothing grays out. Idle stays live with input 2 wired, as Chladni Sand's Idle Strike does, and its help says it acts only without input 2.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). bands selects the seven bands, bands_now smooths them over 1 s (a Filter CHOP) and bands_usual is their usual level (a box Filter CHOP over 4 s). counts selects beat_count, steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises, and strike_ask takes it or Idle's step (idle_steps, from idle_count) for flash_gate(), whose pulses strike_env (a Trigger CHOP) rings down and strikes (a Count CHOP) counts, so each strike goes the other way from the last. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired, source is 0: the generators wander at Idle's level instead of the bands.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The generators' phases move by their drift times the frame's step (absTime.stepSeconds, at most 0.1 s) in phase, so a dropped frame moves them as far as the frames it stood for. The strike's ring is a Trigger CHOP's decay in seconds (0.35), the gate's spacing a Trigger CHOP's re-trigger delay in seconds, the bands' smoothing a Filter CHOP 1 s wide and their usual level a box Filter CHOP 4 s wide, Idle's strikes count absTime.seconds in 2 s steps and its wandering is a sine of absTime.seconds. The Scanimate's sway, ripple and bend and FM's pleats' creep follow lib clock_seconds() (yokes) or absTime.seconds in Python (drive, show's color slide, wrapped).
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 182 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired (Idle's count and wandering following absTime) and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 25 and 29. The phases' feedback only runs while something pulls scan, and yokes takes its width from Lines (the COMP's own parameter), so no expression reads another operator's size. Run again on the build with the vertical yoke's fold guard and the Colorizer's fix: the same, 0 of 182, controls 25 and 29.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  scan and show take the input's format (8-bit from a movie): the raster's light is added up inside one shader in float and eased past white before it is written. phase (7 x 1, the phases, which accumulate every frame) and yokes (Lines x 1, signed shifts in a share of the width and drops in line spacings) are 32-bit float: 8-bit or 16-bit would round a shift of a few pixels or a phase that creeps by 0.002 cycles a frame. levels' and drive's channels feed uniforms, not a data texture.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults, stress beat: GPU 0.89, 0.89, 0.87 ms (95th percentile 1.32, 1.35, 1.31), CPU 0.35 ms each (0.41, 0.40, 0.41), 60 fps held each time; the page gives the medians, 0.89 ms (1.3) and 0.35 ms. Costliest: scan 0.65-0.68 ms (each pixel adds the lines whose spot can reach it, about 25 at the defaults for the Wobbulator's vertical yoke), then yokes, phase and show about 0.1 each. Once each: Scanimate 0.41 ms GPU (scan 0.23), FM 0.73 (scan 0.52, Newton's four steps a line), Lines 720 1.80 (scan 1.56). The last build changed only yokes' vertical yoke (a second sum of seven cosines a line, 240 pixels), not what scan searches. The README DAT says about 1.0 ms.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new scan_processor.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Paik and Abe's Wobbulator (1970) with the Experimental Television Center's documentation (1980); Harrison's Scanimate (1969, its expired patents) with Sieg's SIGGRAPH article (1998); Chowning's FM (JAES 1973, expired patent); Sandin's Image Processor and Scanimate's colorizer for the Colorizer; Fosfora's seven-band split; the red guard shared with VHS + CRT, Bent Cam, Motion Magnify and Slit-Scan; WCAG 2.3.1; and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). The docs page also names the Rutt/Etra Scan Processor and two TouchDesigner versions of it as scan-line effects before this one, which it doesn't follow. Each shader's header says where its idea comes from. Nothing is called original: the beam as spots adding up, a generator a band, the fold guard, the strikes through the gate and Idle are said to be written for this library. The docs page has a Patents paragraph (search 2026-10-28, private/patents/scan_processor.md, board #4743) and names as Not used the song's own waveform along the raster, the yoke that follows the subject and Chebyshev waveshaping, on their looks (board #4744).

Colorizer on the flowers: 1.0/2.0/2.5, red 0, after its fix (a dark hue lifted past 1 is blended toward gray of its brightness, not clipped: clipped, the brightest band's wrap to dark blue dropped brightness and one cell flashed 6.5 a second at Off, area 1.5), measured again with the palette twice over the tones. Dance beat (140 BPM): dancer 1.0/1.5/2.0; flowers 1.0/1.5/2.5, red 0. A song as raw audio (m1x_ease_from_drop): dancer 1.0/1.5/2.0; flowers 1.0/1.5/2.5, red 0. No music (Idle, strikes every 2 s): dancer 1.0 each. - [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: Picture shows the picture's own colors, bent and lit by how the lines bunch. The Colorizer keeps each pixel's brightness and gives it the hue of its band (dark blue, teal, gray, orange, gold, near white), an orange and blue palette that reads in grayscale. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Without Fosfora's feature bus, the bands its bass, melody and drums stand in for are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page (audio_in()'s, as for Chladni Sand and Video Vocoder). Raw audio's bands are measured.
