# relight: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Look and Depth Type (discrete choices) and Flashing; Floats for Brightness, Ambient, Height, Speed, Relief, Roundness, Flatten, Shininess, Specular, Shadows, Softness, Occlusion, Scatter, Glow, Beat Kick, Loudness, Max Distance and Bumps; an Int for Display; a Toggle for Full Screen (a state); Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Beat Kick, Depth Type, Max Distance). Beat Kick's name is Kick and Max Distance's Maxdist; the longest names are Brightness, Fullscreen and Openwindow (10).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: lights reads Look, Brightness, Height, Relief, Beat Kick (times flare_env) and Loudness (through level_smooth, when input 2 is wired); speed reads Speed; near reads Depth Type and Max Distance; near_round's size reads Roundness; bumps reads Bumps; surface reads Relief and Occlusion; shadows reads Shadows and Softness; irradiance reads Glow; sss_h and sss_v read Scatter; show reads Ambient, Specular, Shininess (as the roughness) and Flatten. Values only the inside needs (the looks' table of lights and paths, the lamps' falloff, the shadows' reach and steps, Ambient's and Softness's scales, how far red scatters, the thinness radius, the slope cap, the flare's size and fade) are constants in the builder, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Look, Brightness, Ambient, Height and Speed (the lights); Relief, Roundness, Flatten, Shininess, Specular, Shadows, Softness and Occlusion (the surface); Scatter and Glow (light under the surface); Beat Kick and Loudness (the music); Depth Type, Max Distance and Bumps (input 3); then Flashing and the window. Max Distance grays out unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. Bumps applies only with nothing on input 3, and Beat Kick and Loudness only with something on input 2; they stay live, as no library effect grays a parameter by its wiring, and their help says when they act.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame it rises; flash_limit and flash (lib flash_gate()) pass at most what Flashing allows, and flare_env (a Trigger CHOP) shapes each flare, up at once and fading over 0.6 s. level_pick selects level and level_smooth (a Filter CHOP, 1 s) eases it for the lights' brightness. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired no flares come and the lights keep moving by their own clock.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The lights move by their clock, a Speed CHOP of Speed in seconds (wrapping every 16384 s), on paths whose periods are in seconds; a flare fades over 0.6 s (a Trigger CHOP in seconds) and the loudness is smoothed over a second. Nothing steps from frame to frame (no simulation), so a dropped frame changes nothing but that frame. No frame counts.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 259 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip, its depth and a beat changing every frame wired in; the controls (pulling out_top) cooked 38 and 43. The lights' clock runs only while something pulls the output, and the Switch TOP shape cooks only the depth's path or the stand-in's, never both.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  lights and surface are 32-bit float (places and colors above 1; heights in pixels up to about 180 and normals, which the shadows' march compares closely); relief and bumps are 32-bit mono (nearness and the small differences the bumps stand on); luma, luma8 and near are 16-bit float (linear brightness, nearness); shadows is 8-bit (light let through, 0-1, then smoothed); irradiance and the scattering passes are 16-bit float (light above 1, linear); show is 8-bit, the picture. The Blur TOPs take their inputs' formats.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py, three runs with /tdlib_demo paused (dev/probes/demo_pause.py; MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults (Stage), the dancer's depth on input 3, stress beat: GPU 4.59, 4.65, 4.66 ms (95th percentile 5.36, 5.64, 5.72), CPU 0.48, 0.49, 0.49 ms, 60 fps held each time; the page gives 4.6 ms (5.6) and 0.5 ms. Costliest as listed: shadows_soft 1.25 and shadows 0.93 (the march; a TOP's GPU time can carry another's, dev/probes/blur_cost.py, so read the pair together), near_fine 0.88, show 0.43, sss_h 0.23, surface 0.20, sss_v 0.19. The shadows marched at half the picture's size cost about 1.2 ms more; scattering on every pixel instead of found skin added about 0.5 ms, trimmed to 13 taps a pass. An earlier build under other conditions measured 3.3 ms: the Mac's GPU times wander.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new relight.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name Lambert's diffuse, GGX (Walter et al., EGSR 2007) with Smith's shadowing and Schlick's Fresnel (1994), Luft, Colditz and Deussen's unsharp masking of the depth buffer (SIGGRAPH 2006) with screen-space occlusion's range check, Jimenez et al.'s separable subsurface scattering (CGF 2015) and skin translucency (IEEE CG&A 2010), Jimenez's interleaved gradient noise (SIGGRAPH 2014), the library's depth_near and red guard, WCAG 2.3.1 and the preview footage (CC BY 4.0, AI-generated). The shaders name them where the methods sit. The docs page names the relighting before this one (Prismatic's paid SSDR, phone portrait modes), the patents searched and how the effect keeps clear of the active ones (Canon US 11,948,282, 11,106,938 and 11,037,279, Adobe US 8,379,021, NVIDIA US 8,064,726, Google US 10,916,051 and 12,469,227, Apple US 11,669,985 and 10,528,243), and what was not used (skin found by color, multi-Gaussian profiles, lifting the darks). Original, after a search: the looks and the lights' paths, the depth rounded inward and held to its edges, slopes toward the nearer neighbor, thinness from the height field, the flares on the beat.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + dev/flashcount.py on the final behavior, one run at a time, worst second by the area rule, red where any (clip alone in brackets). Stress beat (8 beats, 2 bars, 1 drop a second), Off / Calm / Up to 3 per second: dancer (depth on input 3) 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 (1.0); flowers (no depth) 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.5, red 0 (1.0, red 1.0); koi 1.0 / 1.5 / 1.0, red 0 (0.5, red 1.5); plaza 0.5 / 1.5 / 1.5 (0.5). At 140 BPM: dancer 1.0 / 1.5 / 3.0 (1.0); hands (depth) 1.0 / 1.0 / 1.5 (1.5); walker (depth) 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 (1.0); night field Off 0 (0). Flashing Off at 140 BPM: Studio, Sun and Two Tone on the dancer and the flowers 1.0 each (1.0); Speed 3 dancer 1.0, flowers 1.0; Height 0.25 1.0; Relief 1 1.0; Brightness 2 with Two Tone 1.0. Off adds nothing to any clip, Calm at most a flash a second, Up to 3 per second reaches WCAG's 3 on the dancer at 140 BPM and no further; red 0 everywhere (the red guard on every pixel took the clips' own out). On the way (board #4677): flares fading over 0.35 s fell fully three times a second and measured 3.5 at Up to 3 per second on the dancer; over 0.6 s they overlap, 2.0.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the lights' colors are decoration, and each look also differs in where its light falls and how hard its shadows are, which read by brightness. Two Tone is amber against blue and Studio warm against cool white; Stage's violet, teal and amber and Sun's warm sun with blue sky are looks, not signals. No text in the output.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Nothing of Relight's own is unreliable: it finds nothing in the picture and every pixel is lit by the same rules. Without input 3 the shape is the picture's brightness, which the help and docs describe as a stand-in, not a measurement. The audio input's own guesses (Fosfora's drops, the raw-audio beat clock) are labeled where the audio input is described.
