# bent_cam: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.2

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Toggles for Song Moments and Full Screen (states); Menus for Byte Slip, Sensor, Mirror, Bursts and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Shorts, Clock Slips, Sync, Compression, Block Errors, Feedback, Zoom, Rotate, Focus, Moiré, Contrast, Saturation, Noise and Length; Ints for Camera, Segments and Display; Pulses for Open Window and Open Docs. Labels in Title Case (Byte Slip, Clock Slips, Block Errors, Song Moments). The longest names are Saturation (10) and Clockslip (9), and the build passes the save-time check. The label Moiré keeps its accent; the name is Moire. 0.1.1 adds Beat Kick, a Float (name Kick), in Title Case.
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: the GLSL uniforms (parent().par.Camera, Shorts, Clockslip, Sync, Compress, Blockerr, Feedback, Zoom, Rotate as radians, Focus as the lens's sigma, Moire, Contrast, Saturation, Noise, Segments, and Byteslip and Mirror by menuIndex), the sensor's size (the screen GLSL's resolution from Sensor, Fit), the lens's blur size (Focus), the burst requests' gains (Bursts, Moments), the burst's hold (Length), Shorts raised by Fosfora's build (Moments), and the gate and budget (Flashing). No script copies values. The lot's table, the screen's pixel pitch and stripes, the sensor's fill, the vignette, the noise's scale, the chroma ceiling, the noise reduction, the JPEG tables and restart interval, the error rates and lifetimes, the slips' share that runs to the line's end, the beat sliver's lines, the burst kinds, the frame jump, the loop's limits and the flash spacing stay in the builder and shaders, off the page. 0.1.1: Beat Kick reaches the bus through an expression on its uniform (Kick x the beat's envelope x the crackle's share); the beat's number reaches bus and dct through expressions too. The crackle's share stays in the builder.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Camera to Sync (the shorts and the bus), Sensor to Block Errors (the resolution and the JPEG), Feedback to Noise (the camera filming its screen), Mirror and Segments, Bursts to Song Moments (the music), then Flashing, then the window. Segments grays out unless Mirror is Kaleidoscope; Length grays out with Bursts Off. Zoom, Rotate, Focus and Moiré stay live at Feedback 0, since they still frame and soften what the camera sees of the screen. 0.1.1: Beat Kick sits in the music section after Length; it isn't grayed out (it acts on every beat, steady ticks included, and Flashing Off stops it, as its help says).
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count, bar_count and drop_count; a time-sliced Slope CHOP (steps) turns each rise into a one-frame step. The burst requests follow bars (or beats) and drops, and the burst's own number (burst_count) picks its kind and seeds its damage, which holds still while it lasts; drop_kind marks a drop's burst (the frame jump). Beat steps drive beat_env, and beat_n (a Count CHOP of them) places each beat's sliver. build is read as a level (Shorts). The beat channel is never read. 0.1.1: beat steps (or steady ticks) drive beat_env, the crackle's envelope, and beat_n, a Count CHOP of them, whose value seeds the sliver and re-places the slips and block errors; the beat channel is still never read.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize. 0.1.1: unchanged.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  Everything follows absTime.seconds. The loop goes round once a camera frame, int(absTime.seconds x 30) worked out in Python (a new frame when it changes, which also seeds the noise), so it turns 30 times a second at 60 fps or 30 (below 30 fps it turns once a TouchDesigner frame). The shorts' touching on and off, the clock slips and the block errors each live for a span of seconds of lib clock_seconds() (wrapped every 16384 s); the steady clock, the beat's envelope (a 0.3 s Trigger CHOP decay), the burst's hold (Length), and the gate and budget (re-trigger delays in seconds) are all in seconds. Nothing counts TouchDesigner's frames. 0.1.1: the crackle fades with beat_env (a 0.3 s Trigger CHOP decay, in seconds) and takes a new scattering each camera frame, int(absTime.seconds x 30) worked out in Python; the slips and errors re-place by the beat's count. Nothing counts frames.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py on the final 0.1.0 build (node viewer off, nothing downstream): 0 of 68 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, with input 2 unwired and with the clip and a beat changing every frame wired in; the control (pulling out_top) cooked 48 and 49. The sensor's size comes from the screen GLSL's Fit resolution, not an expression reading another operator's size, and feed_fb starts from a 1 x 1 constant. 0.1.1: dev/idle.py on the final 0.1.1 build: 0 of 68 operators cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 48 and 49. dev/idle.py on the 0.1.2 build: 0 of 277 operators (the Network Boxes' insides included) cooked over 60 frames unused, unwired and wired; control 48 and 49.
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  The loop (screen, isp, and feed_fb reading isp) is 16-bit float: trails build up and fade over many passes in small steps, which 8 bits would band and stall. camera is mono 16-bit float, one channel of linear light per sensor pixel. bus is 8-bit, as the bytes are (each value exactly n / 255). dct's coefficients are 16-bit float (multiples of their steps, up to a few thousand; 32-bit cost the decoder more bandwidth for nothing). idct and out are 8-bit pictures. still, where feed_fb starts, is 1 x 1 8-bit. No data textures. 0.1.1: unchanged.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the final 0.1.0 build, three runs (MacBook Pro M2 Pro 16 GB, macOS 15.6, TouchDesigner 2025.33230), 1280 x 720, defaults: GPU 1.45, 1.44, 1.37 ms (95th percentile 2.14, 2.17, 2.24), CPU 0.33 ms each, 60 fps held each time; the page gives the median, 1.44 ms GPU (2.2) and 0.33 ms CPU. Costliest: idct 0.8 ms, dct 0.3 ms, bus 0.1 ms. 0.1.1: dev/perf.py on the final 0.1.1 build, three runs: GPU 1.36, 1.44, 1.44 ms (95th percentile 2.05, 2.35, 2.44), CPU 0.34, 0.44, 0.45 ms, 60 fps each; the page gives the medians, 1.44 ms (2.4) and 0.44 ms (0.52), and notes 0.1.0's 0.33 ms CPU. 0.1.2 only lays the network out (Network Boxes, positions), drops the unused compute shaders and holds the README to one line per paragraph: dev/probes/structure_diff.py against 0.1.1 finds every other operator, parameter, expression and wire unchanged, so these numbers stand.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING section and the docs page's Use it section both say to delete the copy and drag in the new bent_cam.tox, or point External .tox at a downloaded file. 0.1.1: unchanged.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  CREDITS and the docs page name dusty_whale's r/vjing post the rig and look come from, linked (the docs page links the author too); circuit bending as Reed Ghazala's practice; video feedback as a dynamical system (Crutchfield, Physica D 10, 1984); YUYV 4:2:2 and BT.601's matrix and ranges; the Bayer mosaic (US patent 3,971,065) and bilinear demosaicing; webcams' temporal noise reduction; JPEG (ITU-T T.81 and its Annex K tables; libjpeg's quality scaling, the IJG's formula; restart markers; DC stored as a difference); lowbias32 (public domain, credited in lib/glsl/common.glsl where it sits, and in lot.py, which mirrors it in Python to draw the lot); what the survey found and wasn't used (elekktronaut's tutorial, circuitBending_MYLES.tox, the Palette's feedback, the Mirror TOP); the beat clock (Dixon 2001, followed); WCAG 2.3.1. The faults' models, the lot, the moiré from the stripes' Fourier series, the chroma ceiling, the bursts and the red guard (shared with VHS + CRT, now in lib/glsl) are written for this library. A scan of the shaders found only lowbias32, the BT.601 matrices, the DCT and its tables, Box-Muller and a stripe's Fourier series. 0.1.1: the crackle (one of D3-D7 flipped on a hashed share of bytes) is written for this library and adds nothing to credit; the jolt and white-balance jump that were tried didn't ship.

- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  Nothing is signaled by color: the false colors are the look, and the bursts carry their timing by changing the whole frame's look and structure. No text is drawn in the effect. The red guard keeps very saturated reds short of WCAG's saturated-red line, inside the loop and on the output. The lot's sheet on the docs page marks each short's kind by shape (triangles down for toward 0, up for toward 1, bars for bridges) as well as by the library's blue and orange. 0.1.1: the crackle is colored static; it carries the beat by its timing, not its hue.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Fosfora's drop detection is marked experimental in the Song Moments help, the README DAT and the docs page; raw-audio beats are marked experimental in the README DAT and the docs page (a kick detector and a beat clock that can still add or miss beats; which beat starts a bar is a guess). 0.1.1: unchanged.
