# photoelastic: idiom review

Reviewed version: 0.1.0

The review items of docs/TD_IDIOMS.md, checked by hand for this version: tick each box
(`- [x]`) and say in a few words how it holds.

- [x] P4 Pulse for actions, Toggle for states, Menu for discrete choices; labels in Title Case. Names of 10 characters or fewer are checked at save time (assert_idiomatic); TouchDesigner itself refuses names that aren't an uppercase letter followed by lowercase letters or digits. [Custom Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Custom_Parameters), [Page Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/Page_Class)
  Menus for Style, Input 3 Holds, Depth Type and Flashing (discrete choices); Floats for Stress, Polarizer Angle, Backlight, Cast, Backdrop, Warm by Movement, Warm by Nearness, Warm by Light, Linger, Sensitivity, Max Distance, Beat Squeeze, Loudness and Bar Turn; a Toggle for Full Screen; Pulses for Reset, Open Window and Open Docs; an Int for Display. Labels in Title Case (Warm by Movement, Input 3 Holds keep their small words lower case). The longest names are Openwindow, Fullscreen, Depthtype and Warmlight (10 or fewer; the save check passes).
- [x] P5 Inner operators follow the COMP's parameters through expressions (`parent().par.Amount`), not scripts copying values; values only the inside needs are kept off the pages (`ipar`). [Internal Parameters](https://docs.derivative.ca/Internal_Parameters)
  Every inner operator follows the page through expressions: look reads Style, Stress (times the load: Loudness with level_smooth, Beat Squeeze with squeeze_env, while input 2 is wired), Backdrop, Backlight, Cast and Polarizer Angle (plus Bar Turn times turn_lag); chart reads Style; subject reads Warm by Nearness (only while input 3 is wired) and Warm by Light; warmth reads Warm by Movement and Linger; find reads Input 3 Holds and Sensitivity, near Depth Type and Max Distance. The Parameter Execute DAT reset only pulses warmth_fb on Reset, copying no values. Values only the inside needs (the orders per unit of stress and their cap, the chart's size and range, the tint plate's retardation, the pyramid's levels and Jacobi passes, the warmth's thresholds and times, the squeeze's size and length, the turn's gap and easing, the loudness easing) are constants in the builder or the shaders, off the page.
- [x] P6 Related parameters are grouped with `startSection`; parameters that don't apply in the current mode are grayed out with `enableExpr`.
  Sections: Style, Stress, Polarizer Angle, Backlight, Cast and Backdrop (the polariscope); Warm by Movement, Warm by Nearness, Warm by Light, Linger, Input 3 Holds, Sensitivity, Depth Type, Max Distance and Reset (what warms the plastic, input 3); Beat Squeeze, Loudness and Bar Turn (the music); then Flashing and the window. Polarizer Angle and Bar Turn gray out unless Style is Plane or Tint Plate, Backdrop when it is Light Field, Max Distance unless Depth Type is Distance in Meters. The input-3 and music parameters stay live with nothing wired, as in every library effect; their help says they act on input 3 or input 2.
- [x] I4 The audio input goes through `audio_in()`, which reads it by channel name with fallbacks and takes a pulse from its count channel when one exists, because a one-frame pulse is lost on any frame nothing pulls the CHOP; the effect reads the counts (`beat_count`, `bar_count`...) for logic, not `beat`. [Cook](https://docs.derivative.ca/Cook), [Logic CHOP](https://docs.derivative.ca/Logic_CHOP)
  Input 2 goes through audio_in(). counts selects beat_count and bar_count, and steps (a time-sliced Slope CHOP) is above 0 on the frame each rises. Beats go through flash_gate() (ask, flash_limit, flash) to squeeze_env. Bars go through bar_gap (a Trigger CHOP, at most one every 2 s) to bars (a Count CHOP: the turns since the effect started, so wiring a signal in mid-song doesn't spin the polarizers through every bar so far) and turn_lag (eased 1.5 s). level_smooth eases the loudness over a second. The one-frame beat channel is never read. With nothing wired the audio terms fall away (audio's source is 0): an even load, no squeeze, no turn.
- [x] E2 Only capitalized members are promoted; state lives on `self` and the extension can be reinitialized at any time; no module globals; `onInitTD`/`onDestroyTD`, the 2025 names. [Release Notes 2025](https://docs.derivative.ca/Release_Notes/2025.30000)
  No extension in this effect, so nothing to promote or reinitialize.
- [x] C4 Motion and timing follow seconds (`absTime.seconds`, `me.time.seconds`), not frame counts, so dropped frames don't change the speed. [AbsTime Class](https://docs.derivative.ca/AbsTime_Class)
  The warmth rises and falls by the frame's step (lib warmth(), absTime.stepSeconds) and holds movement from the input's new frames; squeeze_env, bar_gap, turn_lag and level_smooth are in seconds; the unwired light circles on absTime through math.sin. The stress is solved afresh each frame and carries no time of its own. No shader takes the clock.
- [x] C5 Nothing cooks while the output is unused: checked with `dev/idle.py` (nothing downstream, the node out of view; input 2 unwired and wired to a changing beat), or in the Performance Monitor. [Performance Monitor](https://docs.derivative.ca/Performance_Monitor_Dialog)
  dev/idle.py: unwired 0 of 308 operators cooked over 60 frames unused (control 52 cooked), wired to the clip and the stress beat 0 of 308 (control 64).
- [x] C6 Pixel formats are 8-bit unless precision is needed, and 32-bit float for data textures (a CHOP to TOP feeding a shader).
  The look's output is 8-bit. The warmth's pyramid and the potential's levels are 32-bit float (mono): the potential runs to hundreds, and its second differences need the precision. The stress is 32-bit float, subject, near, find and the chart 16-bit float data, as are lib warmth()'s and optical_flow()'s own.
- [x] C7 The performance note on the docs page was measured on the reference machine at 1280 x 720, not estimated.
  dev/perf.py on the M2 Pro at 1280 x 720, the dancer with her depth and the stress beat, the demo paused: GPU 1.91, 1.95, 2.19 ms (median 1.95, 95th percentile 3.3), CPU 0.67 ms, 60 fps held. The docs page and README give these.
- [x] D5 The README DAT and docs page say how to update: dragging a `.tox` in embeds a copy, so updating means dragging in the new one (or pointing External .tox at it). [A word about external files](https://derivative.ca/community-post/word-about-external-files-202310k-builds/68166)
  The README DAT's UPDATING and the docs page's Use it both say dragging a .tox in embeds a copy, so updating means deleting it and dragging in the new photoelastic.tox, or pointing External .tox at a downloaded copy.
- [x] D6 Provenance: the README DAT's CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name every paper, technique, project, code, font, model and media the effect uses, follows or was built after, with links and licenses, and call original only what was designed here and has no earlier source after a search. Code recalled from elsewhere that can't be traced is replaced, never called original; code taken from elsewhere is also credited where it sits (a comment in the shader or script). D3 and check_tox.py only see that the sections exist.
  README CREDITS and the docs page's Credits and license name Brewster 1816, Maxwell 1853, Frocht 1941, Michel-Levy and Sorensen 2013, Wyman, Sloan and Shirley 2013 (lib spectrum.glsl), Jones 1941, Goodier 1937, Bornemann and Deuflhard 1996, Burt and Adelson 1983, Spitzer 1964, the library's warmth, optical_flow, depth_near, depth_find, common and red_guard, Dixon 2001 for the beat clock and WCAG 2.3.1, each checked against Crossref or its DOI. The Not used entries name Dolby US 8,929,654 and US 9,077,942, ByteDance US 12,694,597, GritWorld US 2023/0106802 and Adobe US 11,409,423 and US 11,755,187 (private/patents/photoelastic.md), and the docs page names the stress from curvature and from the flow's strain rate that the prototype set aside. Original: the picture's warmth as the plastic's heating, the cast, backlight, backdrop, squeeze and turn; no earlier photoelastic effect taking a picture was found.
- [x] A2 The flash limits were measured, not assumed: the output recorded with a beat-heavy input at each Flashing setting, and the flashes in any one second counted.
  dev/flash.py + flashcount.py under the stress beat, each Flashing setting: dancer 1.5/1.5/2.0 (clip 1.0), flowers 1.0/1.5/2.0 (clip 1.0, red 1.0; the effect no red), koi 1.0/1.0/1.0 (clip 0.5, red 1.5; no red), plaza 0.5/1.0/1.0 (clip 0.5), hands 2.0/1.5/1.5 (clip 1.5); the dancer at Calm in Light Field 1.5, Plane 1.0, Tint Plate 1.0, and with Stress, Beat Squeeze and Loudness all at 1 2.0. Every one within its setting's rule; the docs page's table gives them.
- [x] A3 No meaning rests on hue alone (brightness, size or motion carries it too); the palette is colorblind-safe (orange and blue); text is legible at the size it is shown.
  The fringes' colors follow the stress and so does their brightness (Michel-Levy's chart swings from black through white). Tint Plate's direction reads as blue against yellow, a colorblind-safe pair that also differs in brightness; Plane says the direction by dark bands. No text is drawn.
- [x] A4 Anything unreliable is labeled experimental in its help and docs.
  Raw audio's beat clock is labeled experimental in the README DAT and the docs page's Experimental section; the effect has nothing else unreliable to label.
